# Brief: cover for {{TITLE}}

Design a Kindle cover (1600 × 2560 px, legible as a 160 px thumbnail) for **{{TITLE}}** by
{{AUTHOR}}, an interactive {{GENRE}} novel. Read BIBLE.md §1, §4, §5 first.

- One image, one idea: the entity or its sign in the book's setting, made slightly wrong. Don't show
  the monster's face; show one detail the reader will recognise at the right moment in the book.
- Title readable at thumbnail size: big, high contrast, max two typefaces. Author name at the bottom.
  Optional small "AN INTERACTIVE HORROR NOVEL" line.
- Deliver as self-contained HTML/SVG (`templates/cover.html`, no external images; Google Fonts allowed)
  or a Python script that writes that HTML (procedural art, seeded so it is reproducible — the
  Layover's v2 cover was a two-ink risograph-style SVG generated by script). Use `{{author}}` and
  `{{title}}` placeholders.
- Render with `python3 -m engine {{BOOK_DIR}} cover`, look at the PNG at full size and at 160 px wide,
  and iterate until both read. Then set `epub.cover` to the output.

Show the author 2–3 directions as thumbnails before finishing one.
