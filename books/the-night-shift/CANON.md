## The single true account of the two requests

1. **Eight years ago** (2018, after Dad's funeral), Mum told **Ros**: *"Go. Take the job. If you stay, your sister will
   let you do everything and never have a life."* Ros was **sent**, and let you think she left.
2. **Six years ago** (2020, after the first stroke), Mum told **you**: *"Stay with me. Don't let them put me anywhere."*
   You were **asked**, and let Ros think you chose.
3. **Three years ago** (2023) Mum named Ros on the LPA, with a letter to you, *For Bean*: *"Because you would never let me
   go, love, and I couldn't make you be the one."*

Ros knows 1 and 3. You know 2. Neither knows the other's request until a MENDED scene. The reader may learn them in either
order, or never. Mum never asked either of you to keep it secret; you each kept it because it made you the good one.

## Rules of the place (as the NIGHT STAFF NOTICE prints them, A23)

1 Visiting hours are over. Do not tell anyone you are a visitor. 2 If the Night Sister calls you by a patient's name, do
not answer. 3 Never lie down on a ward bed. 4 After 3 a.m. the lifts go down only. 5 Ward 0 is closed. 6 Do not sign
anything. 7 If the lights dim to night setting, keep walking. 8 (torn off; found as THE LAST RULE: *Let them go.*)

What the Sister cannot do: follow you through a door you were told never to open (she is already inside Ward 0, R06);
read a name spoken by someone who loves you (H18, W28/W31, R14, R35); keep anyone who has been let go (R35).
