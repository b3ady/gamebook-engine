# THE NIGHT SHIFT, Round 2, Reviewer A (craft and continuity)

## 1. Verdict: 8.5/10 (prev: 7.5)

Round 1's structural faults are mostly fixed, and the fixes read well. Anger now earns SPOKE (§63 "Then say the rest"). The four-way "two requests" scene is one canonical scene (Legion sink, hat on, good hand round the wrist, the three-way sister/brother/our-kid line in all four places). E_TRUE no longer contradicts itself, and the ring, the glasses, the stairs and the birth minute are all consistent. The book's remaining problem is that Ros's arc still lands early, because §169 W21 tells the whole secret on a first Old Wing visit. The tic cull is also only half done. A third group is small reader-conditional falsehoods (§152, §160, §52) and tabard continuity. Fix 1-4 and it is a 9.

## Ranked problems (new or remaining)

1. **§169 W21 gives away the whole secret at about 30%.** Quote: "'I didn't leave,' Ros says. 'I was sent.'" Both requests are told verbatim in the chapel, gated only by SPOKE + HELD, both cheap. After that B09, M22 and R41 are pointers or echoes, and the confession-driven part of the Ros arc is spent before the Basement. Fix: stop W21 at the two-line exchange ("She asked." / "She sent me.") with no verbatim text. Keep Ros's quoted line, the sink and the "wasn't needed" line for whichever of B09/M22/R41 is reached first. W21 then gives Lose 1 Fear only. The "told each other" pointers already exist, so they would still work.
2. **Ros's trust is still bought with free beats (§120 A10, §69 A28, §81 W31, §104 R15).** Quote: "Note the codeword SPOKE." A10 is a refusal ("I'm not going to tell you") that earns the "first honest exchange" flag. A28 gives HELD with no cost, while the A29 alternative costs Vigour and Fear. The rescues W31 and R15 cost nothing. Fix: remove SPOKE from A10, since H04, W19 and A34 give it for real. Give A28 "Gain 1 Fear" (her teeth, your palm). Give W31/R15 "Lose 1 Vigour" (hauling her, getting her shoes on).
3. **Reader-conditional falsehoods.**
   - §152 H03: "the first time you've seen her sit since she came through the family-room door". It is false for anyone who took §54 A08, where "She sits down on the other settee". Fix: "the first time you've seen her sit without her bag on her knee."
   - §160 A36: "You don't remember it going on." This follows §198 A14, where the band appears and Ros asks "Since when have you had that?" Fix: gate the sentence on not having had the tea.
   - §52 R10: "nobody's claimed you yet." This is false if M09, W27 or R12 has already crossed a field off. Fix: add "*If you have crossed a field off,*" 'Somebody's been at it,' she says, still not looking up."
4. **Captioned subtext, second wave.** The tell is "[image]. [Sentence saying what the image means]."
   - §26 W19: "Ros does not say things she hasn't checked first." Also: "So she has been counting too. You didn't know anyone was counting but you."
