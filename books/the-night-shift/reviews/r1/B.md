# THE NIGHT SHIFT: Round 1 review B (horror fan + game flow)

## 1. Verdict: 7.5/10

A genuinely frightening, emotionally exact book wrapped in a hub structure that over-explains, under-escalates and re-reads itself. Would I recommend it? Yes, to a horror reader who likes to cry. The first 40 sections and the Ward 0 reveal are close to 9. The middle is a fetch-quest checklist, and the Night Sister's pursuit is promised more than it is played.

Based on route09 (true ending, 131 reads) and route11 (Admitted, 92 reads), plus FRONT_INTRO, FRONT_RULES and BIBLE.

## Ranked problems

1. **§98 (also §171, §107, §17, §87): the hubs are re-read in full every visit.** "Four lines run along the floor, laid in the seventies and worn pale down the middle". In route09 the Main Corridor is read 7 times (about 330 words each), §17 six times, §107 five times, §171 four times. "Go straight to the choices" still means scrolling past the whole block. This is the main pacing killer.
   Fix: make each hub a short return section (about 60 words plus choices) and move the one-time description into a first-arrival section reached only from the lift, the stairs or the linen route. Do this for 98, 171, 107, 17 and 87.

2. **§98 (the clock and rounds): the Night Sister never escalates on the page.** "the red torch is one ward nearer". The BIBLE promises a round every hour, each closer to your bed. In play she appears at §99 (Level 3), §62 (scripted 2nd return) and Ward 0. Rounds 3 to 5 have no scene, and ticks are easily kept at 3 by touring each wing once. The 06:00 Admitted threat never bites.
   Fix: give §98's return text a variant per ticked box (03:00 trolley parked at the tea bar, 04:00 the Sister sitting at the hatch with your folder, 05:00 the corridor already on night setting). Let one or two non-travel actions tick a round, such as resting or the tea bar.

3. **§156 (also §44, §132, §130, §101): the rule is quoted right before the choice, so these are compliance quizzes.** "Rule 6 on the Notice: *Do not sign anything.*" Sign, answer, lie down and stop-and-wait are all labelled by the rule one paragraph above, and the safe option is always free. §100's free exit (§183) and §46's "Just waiting for someone" are the same shape.
   Fix: delete the in-section rule quotes except where obeying is tempting to break. Make obeying cost something at least three times (rule 7 means leaving Ros behind in §44; the safe option at §100 needs the badge).

4. **§100 and §28: the Nightingale ward round is a free pass, and the options are muddled.** "If you keep walking for the far doors, turn to 183." Seven beds away, nothing to test, and §183 is cost-free. The badge/tabard line says "Either way, turn to 165" while the next line also sends "stand at a bedside like a visitor" to §78. Which is the no-badge version? §28 says the tabard lets you "do the round. Turn to 87", which is the hub the reader just came from. That is almost certainly a wrong target.
   Fix: make §100 a real set piece (a Nerve test or a Fear cost for walking). Split the bedside option cleanly ("with the badge, §165; without it, §78"). Check §28's target (165?).

5. **§119: the Ward 0 stair door is a repeatable, deterministic fatal option, and its penalty is ambiguous.** "Fill in 1 more field on your wristband." The text has just said the WARD field filled itself, so a reader may fill two. The hub still offers the door after you've died there (route11 uses it three times). It teaches nothing the second time.
   Fix: print "the WARD field is your death's field; fill nothing else". Add "if you haven't tried the door" to the hub line. Consider a Nerve test to climb back, so the dread has a mechanic.

6. **§178 (and every Level 3 death): the death returns to §85, not the §157 checkpoint, and Fear re-accumulates.** "Return to 85 and choose again." In route09 Fear is 3 before the death and 5 after replaying §65, §109 and §92. The rules say Level 3 deaths "cost nothing" and the sheet is "as it was", but replay adds fresh Fear. The ✈ at §157 is ignored.
   Fix: return to the last ✈ (§157), and add to the rules that Fear and Vigour revert to their checkpoint values.

7. **§16 and §144: Ros's scissors and the other "cross off" lines are gated on codewords, not on filled fields.** "Cross off 1 field on your Wristband. Lose 1 Vigour." Route11 offers §16 with zero fields filled; the sheet goes to "Wristband -1". §29 ("one of the fields ... has gone blank") and §102 have the same hole.
   Fix: add "if at least one field is filled" to the gates, with a fallback ("nothing to cut; she puts the scissors away, unspent").

8. **§161: an undocumented three-field penalty.** "If three or more fields of your Wristband are filled, the porters have seen your wrist." FRONT_RULES give thresholds of 4 (true ending closed) and 5 (Admitted) only. A reader at three fields thinks they're safe and then loses their choice.
   Fix: move the threshold to 4, or state "three fields: the Kept can see it" in FRONT_RULES under The Wristband.

9. **§56/§196 (finale): the true climax has almost no jeopardy.** The Sister is passive at the bedside. The obs-chart walk costs Fear +2 and a Fortune test, with no visible round closing. §158 says "the round is a bay nearer" and then nothing follows. The wrong smile on Mum and the rudely vivid "pat the bed" are the only dread.
   Fix: make wrong turns advance the torch bay by bay (three wrong turns means the Sister reaches the bed and you go to Admitted). Keep the passive Sister for §56 only.

10. **§106: the reveal is explained rather than felt.** "Every one of them was kind to you once. She has kept the kindness and thrown the rest away." The faces-assembled image is great, but the sentence spells out its thesis.
    Fix: cut the last two sentences. Let §196's "they're only nurses at the end of a long night" do the explaining.

11. **§196: the dissolve is too gentle for the climax of a horror book.** The nurses "go off down the ward together, chatting" and Mum-at-24 winks. Lovely, but there is no counter-attack and no cost beyond Mum's face falling.
    Fix: before the faces step out, give the Sister one last, genuinely frightening offer, such as all her voices saying "Maureen" together and the cap lifting off. Keep the wink.

12. **§37 and FRONT_RULES, the Notice: rules spelled out, and "Believe them." is false.** "There are seven of them. There used to be eight. Believe them." Rule 5 (Ward 0 is closed) must be broken (§31: "the one you have to break"), and rule 6 is dismissed at §101 as "written in 1979 by somebody who had never had to sit outside theatre". Rule 1 (don't say you're a visitor) fights the VISITOR'S BADGE that is the key item.
    Fix: change FRONT_RULES to "Believe them, until you have a reason not to." Consider showing only rules 1 to 4 at §37, with 5 to 7 on a second card later. Resolve rule 1 against the badge, for example by "VISITOR" being what the Kept will accept you as.

13. **§198, §95 and §168: accepting care has no real temptation.** "Gain 2 Vigour. Fill in 1 field on your Wristband: NAME." The rewards are Vigour +2, +3 and +4, and Vigour is almost never under threat (one fight in either route, Story Mode). So the experienced reader always refuses and the kindness-is-the-weapon theme is untested mechanically. Nerve and Vigour are decorative; Fear and fields do all the work.
    Fix: pay for care in the stats that matter, such as Fear -3, or an immediate escape from a death or a clue. Then the field is a real cost.

14. **§76 and §189 (Fear economy): Fear sits at 10 to 11 of 12 by mid-book.** In route09 Fear reaches +11 after the porters' lodge, with the limit at 12, and one more "Gain 1 Fear" would admit you with no test and no warning. Drains (luggage tag -2, Gary's mother -2, Mum's voice -2) are scattered and unflagged.
    Fix: add a banner at hub §98: "If your Fear is within 2 of its limit, read the tea bar and the chapel." Or give a Nerve test before the final point. Consider capping per-scene gains.

15. **§127/§170: leaving the property sack silently deletes GLASSES and TICKET.** "When you turn round, the chair is empty." The reader's very human "I can't open it yet" costs two items and +1 Fear, and nothing says so. The items then pay off only as flavour lines in §200 (glasses, ticket, ring). The BIBLE says returning them matters in endings, but nothing gates on them.
    Fix: let the sack reappear at Property or Lost Property, or tell the reader on §170 ("It will be gone by morning"). Make one item mechanical (the ring gating §38, or the glasses helping at the charts, §123).

16. **§98 / §180 / §62 / §68: the round tick is stated in four places.** §180 and §62 each say "Tick one round", §98 has an overlay "if you have turned here straight from ...", and §32/§70/§68 branch on RETURNED/OFFERED. The 06:00 check lives only in §98 ("turn to the section your rules give for it"). A reader coming §180 → §152 → §98 may double-tick.
    Fix: put "Tick one round" in each return section (§68, §24, §132/§32, §70) before branching, delete it from §98, and print the Admitted number in the text instead of "the section your rules give".

17. **§173 to §84: the obs chart is not checkable.** "Every entry is timed at or before the hour on the station clock." No hour is ever printed, so the reader can't check any entry. The only wrong-making clues are blatant (§45 your name, §48 your date of birth in Mum's chart and the chart in the wrong bay). §158's Fortune test also flips an innocent chart to a wrong one at random.
    Fix: print the station clock time at §173. Add one chart with a time that hasn't happened yet (in the BIBLE, unused). Keep §48 as the capstone.

18. **§149 and §125: Act 1 choices converge without leaving a mark.** Hug or handover both reach §125, and "I do", "Ask her" and "We both do" all end at the settee. No codeword or Fear is set (route09 reaches §127 with a clean sheet), so the surgeon's question, the book's central question, does not echo later.
    Fix: set a one-word flag per answer (e.g. STAYED, DEFERRED, TOGETHER) and have §153 and §196 pick up a line from it.

19. **§193 (the Admitted ending): no end marker or restart line.** It stops at "somebody comes to see how you are." Route09 closes with THE END and the Readmitted pointer ("If you know the minute you were born, turn to it").
    Fix: verify that the harness has not cut the tail. Add "THE END" and the back-to-the-beginning instruction. Make §193's opening text distinguish the cause (fields / rounds / Fear), because "Your Wristband is full, or the last hour has come round, or you're simply too frightened" is a shrug.

20. **Continuity nits.**
    - §102 says "an ordinary Thursday noise" and §77 says "a Friday in November".
    - Three Okafors: J. on the rota (§89), D. in Toronto (§153), Mr in Bay 5 (§48).
    - §196: "A&E at nine o'clock last night", yet it is 02:00 and the ambulance was tea-time.
    - §139 uses "If you have a cup, turn to 95" as if it were a conditional.
    - FRONT_RULES contains unresolved `{E_ADMITTED}` placeholders; confirm they are replaced by 193 in print.
