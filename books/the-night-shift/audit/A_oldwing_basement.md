# Audit: Old Wing and Basement (A_oldwing_basement)

Counts: HIGH 3, MED 9, LOW 12.

## HIGH

- [HIGH] §174 B09: "*If you and Ros have already heard each other out, in Records or on Ward 9,*" — dead branch. B09 is only reachable with "has SPOKE and lacks MENDED", and Records/Ward 9 scenes give MENDED, so nobody sees it. The readers who really arrive having told Ros are the chapel readers from §169 W21 (they gain ASKED and SENT but not MENDED). They get the "Otherwise" branch, and Ros looks up as if hearing "She asked me" for the first time. — Fix: replace the opening italic with "*If you have already begun this in the chapel,* you don't say it twice. 'Later,' Ros said, and this is later. She nods at the form in her hands: the third thing, the one that came after. Then read on from *Either way.*"
- [HIGH] §153 B08: "Three years. Three Christmases on the phone." — false. Act 1 (A01/A36) says you were last in a room together "Christmas before last, at Mum's" (2024), after the 2023 LPA. — Fix: "Three years. Every phone call. Every *fine*."
- [HIGH] §78 W12 and §35 B20: "Fill in 1 more field on your Wristband: WARD" / "…: DATE OF BIRTH" — undecidable when the named field is already filled. DOB is also named at H11 (tea bar) and WARD at D08, so a reader who took H11 and then B20 is told to tick a field already ticked, and the rules page says only "If the book names the field, tick that one". — Fix, in FRONT_RULES after "if it doesn't, tick the next empty one": "If the field the book names is already filled, tick the next empty one instead." Then the count always rises by 1.

## MED

- [MED] §179 LOCKER: "'That went with a form,' she says. 'Health and welfare. It names me" — if LOCKER comes before Records, Ros tells you the LPA's content and that she signed it. B07 then says "a form you have never seen" and B08 repeats "I signed it too" with you surprised ("You knew"). Canon says the first telling must be at B07/LOCKER only. — Fix: make the Ros paragraph conditional. "*If Ros is with you and you already have MUM'S LETTER,* she looks at the envelope, not at you. 'That went with a form,' she says. 'Page eleven. I signed it too.' *If Ros is with you and you did not,* she looks at the envelope, not at you, and says nothing. Either way she touches the blur at the end of the row in the photo with one fingertip, and takes it away fast."
- [MED] §7 B16 and §179 LOCKER: "Note the RING." — B14 has you put the ring on your little finger, but B16 and LOCKER leave it in your fist/pocket. R39 says "You slide the ring off your little finger", which is false for B16/LOCKER readers. — Fix, B16: before "Note the RING." add "You put it on your own little finger, the only one it will go over." LOCKER: change "You hold the ring in your fist until your hand stops shaking." to "You hold the ring in your fist until your hand stops shaking, then put it on your little finger, the only one it will go over."
- [MED] §100 W10 / §183 W13: "Thirty arms come up off thirty blankets" — W02 says 30 beds, the door bed is empty, and W10 itself says one more is empty. So there are at most 28 Kept. W13 repeats "Thirty people in. Thirty people out." Also "the Kept" is used here without having been introduced in the text. — Fix W10: "And every one of them, the Kept, holds out a wrist. Arm after arm comes up off the blankets and turns palm up together, on one beat, and holds there." W10 later: "Halfway down the ward a second bed has nobody in it." W13: "Everyone in. Everyone out. All in time."
- [MED] §32 B30: "You count eleven flights… On the twelfth landing" — flights and landings disagree: you pass a landing every two flights, so eleven flights is five and a half landings, and the twelfth landing is flight 24. — Fix: "You count twenty-two flights." and "On the eleventh landing there's a door".
- [MED] §68 W29 / §24 W30: W30 says "the ward lights drop to night setting: the Nightingale ward first", but W02 says the ward is "already on night setting". False for any reader who went into the ward. — Fix W30: "Behind you, bank by bank, the lights go down another step, to a dim amber: the Nightingale ward first, then the chapel, then the strip lights along the tiles, each one dimming as you pass beneath it, so that the dark follows a step behind your heels all the way to the doors."
- [MED] §55 W22: "Somewhere in there a boy's mum. You don't look for her." — W22 is open to readers who have not met Gary (the office can be visited before the ward). — Fix: "Somewhere in there, you think, is every visitor who ever waited. You don't look."
- [MED] §199 D04: "It's in her voice, from somewhere behind the altar" — "her" has no antecedent. The only women in the section are Mum (just written "For Mum") and Ros. The Sister may not have been met yet. — Fix: "It's in the Night Sister's voice, from somewhere behind the altar…"
- [MED] §97 D03: "Somewhere down the ward a phone is ringing. Somebody should get that." — D03 is also reached from W10 by readers whose Ros is with them and whose phone is silent, so nothing is ringing. W28 readers are in a side room, not "the ward". — Fix: "Somewhere a phone is ringing. Somebody should get that. In a minute." (generic and true for both)
- [MED] §44 B17, §146 B18, §141 D06, §12 B19, §35 B20: no "If Ros is with you" line for the whole mortuary sequence, though she is with you in the Basement (B01 onward). She vanishes for these sections and is back at B01. — Fix, B17 (after "Night setting."): "*If Ros is with you,* she stops at the mouth of the corridor. 'No,' she says. 'I'll be at the lift. Shout.' She means it, and you go on alone." (B01 already tolerates her being back.)
