# Audit: Level 3 + Main Corridor (printed numbers; STATE.md incoming links checked)

## HIGH

- [HIGH] §189 A33: "You press the button again. The numbers don't move." — A32 arrivals never pressed the button (A32 only says the call button is lit), so "again" is false. A31 arrivals left a trolley following "one room behind" with Ros watching it; A33 drops it and Ros is suddenly at the window with her back to it. — Fix: replace the first two sentences with "You press the button, though it is already lit. The numbers don't move. *If the trolley followed you,* it has stopped at the mouth of the lobby, and Ros has turned her back on it. That is how much she needs to say this."

## MED

- [MED] §150 A06: "Every other person in this building has written down everything you've said" — false for every arriving reader: the Sister has not appeared yet (A13 comes after A09), A03 readers watched Ros not type a word, and the theatre nurse and surgeon wrote nothing. — Fix: "He doesn't write anything down. That is what you notice, in a building where everything you say seems to get written down."
- [MED] §95 H11: "nobody has made you a cup of tea in six years that you didn't make first" — false for any reader who took the Sister's tea at A14 (§198), and A14 itself says it is "the first thing anyone has offered you". — Fix: "...and because it's the middle of the night, and because for six years you have been the one who makes the tea."
- [MED] §69 A28: "Her teeth have found the heel of your palm; the mark stays." — Ros is described as "rigid... held, waiting", and the bite appears only after you take your hand away, so it reads as new action with no cause. A33 relies on the hand-over-mouth moment. — Fix: "In the first second, before she understood, her teeth found the heel of your palm; the mark stays. Gain 1 Fear. Note the codeword HELD."
- [MED] §157 A24: "Rule 7 sits in the middle of the card, the one about the lights." — Rule 7 is the last printed rule (the card tears off under it, A23), not the middle. — Fix: "Rule 7 sits last on the card, just above the torn edge, the one about the lights."
- [MED] §125 A05: "The surgeon is still in his paper hat, between cases" — he is mid-operation on Mum ("We're about halfway"), not between cases. — Fix: "The surgeon is still in his paper hat, scrubbed out for five minutes, with his mask pulled down under his chin."
- [MED] §94 A20: "Everything is where it should be. You think." — For readers from A19 (§65), the trolley was left "square on" at the far end, "waiting"; A20 shows that end (the window) and never mentions it. — Fix: "*If the trolley is still at the far end,* you don't look at it. Everything else is where it should be. You think."
- [MED] §178 D01 / §37 A23: "Return to {A17} and choose again." then "You peel the card off the door." — D01 is only reachable after the card is already in your pocket; the return to A17 replays A23, so the reader peels it again and writes the Notice a second time. — Fix in A23: "You peel the card off the door. If it is not already on your Adventure Sheet, write the Night Staff Notice there. Its rules are printed there too. You'll want them by heart."
- [MED] FRONT_RULES (Codewords): "*If Ros is with you,*" is used all through Level 3 and the Corridor but the rules never say what it means; the only definition is in H02 ("you have the codeword WARDED"). — Fix: add to "Codewords and items": "When the book says *If Ros is with you,* it means you do not have the codeword WARDED. When you do, Ros is on the Old Wing and the book will say so."

## LOW

- [LOW] §42 HLD: "If you do not have the codeword RETURNED, climb back... turn to {H02}." — HLD is only reachable at Rounds >= 3, so RETURNED and OFFERED are always held (H02 and H05 are always visited by the 2nd return). The first two branches are dead, and neither ticks a round. — Fix: delete them, leaving: "Climb back to the Main Corridor, tick one round on your Adventure Sheet, and turn to {H01}."
- [LOW] §130 HL: "The doors close. The lift thinks about it." — after 03:00 HL is a free round-burner for a reader who ignores Notice rule 4, and H01's bullet offers the lift without a reminder. — Fix (in H01): "To take the lift, turn to {HL} (after 03:00 the lifts go down only). To take the stairs, turn to {HS}."
- [LOW] §189 A33: "*If you have the codeword HELD,* she rubs her mouth where your hand was" — HELD persists after a D02 return to A24, so a reader who chose A29 (let her answer) this time still gets the line. — Fix: "*If you put your hand over her mouth,* she rubs her mouth where your hand was..."
- [LOW] §160 A36: "*Otherwise,* there's a band on it. White, soft" — "it" is unclear (wrist or sleeve), and the E_LOOP reader already has 1 field filled and so is not "waiting to be filled in". — Fix: "*Otherwise,* there's a band on your wrist. White, soft, the hospital kind, fitted snug. Five printed fields: NAME. DATE OF BIRTH. NEXT OF KIN. WARD. BED. Each with its own small box. You don't remember it going on."
- [LOW] §73 A11: "A lottery ticket, folded twice." — A09 promised "the corner of something pink" and A12 says "whatever the pink corner was"; A11 never resolves it. — Fix: "A lottery ticket, pink, folded twice."
- [LOW] §37 A23: blank line between "4. After 3 a.m. the lifts go down only." and "5. Ward 0 is closed." — stray break in the printed list (every other rule is tight). — Fix: delete the blank line.
- [LOW] §152 H03: "Ros sits down on the bench outside the chapel." — H02 ends with her filming the steel doors, and H01 names no bench, so there is no bridge. — Fix: "Ros walks as far as the chapel door and sits down on the bench beside it."
- [LOW] §98 H01: "A chapel door with a cross of coloured glass." — no choice reaches this door, and the Old Wing sign points to a different CHAPEL (W14); LEAD_NOTES flagged this. — Fix: "A chapel door with a cross of coloured glass, locked from the corridor side."
- [LOW] FRONT_RULES (Checkpoints and death): "going back costs nothing. Level 3 deaths cost no field." — the two sentences say the same thing. — Fix: delete "Level 3 deaths cost no field."
