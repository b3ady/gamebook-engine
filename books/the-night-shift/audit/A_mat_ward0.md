# Audit A: Maternity, Ward 0, Ending (fine-tooth, per 06_audit.md)

Counts: HIGH 2, MED 15, LOW 7 (24 bullets). Typography scan (ellipses, dashes, quotes, double spaces, a/an, doubled words) of all scoped sections found nothing.
Wristband arithmetic checked: R02 (>=3 porters / <=2 choose), R30 (>=4 to R31, <=3 to R32/R33), R08/R09 vs Rounds <=4 / >=5, M09/R08/R11/R12 cross-offs, D07/D08/D09/D10 +1. Rounds are always 3 to 5 on arrival at Ward 0 (badge, letter and rule come from three different zones), so "three o'clock" on the Bay 4 chart is always a past hour. Obs-chart loop (R16, R18, R19, R20, R22, R23, R24, R28, R25-27, R30): every exit is wired correctly and terminates (R22 gives 2 Fear each fold, so Fear limit ends it).

## HIGH

- [HIGH] §8 M22 and §47 R41 (same phrase also in §174 B09): "in Records or on Ward 9" — the skip branches ("neither of you tells it again" / "you don't tell her that part again") can only fire for a reader who holds ASKED+SENT but lacks MENDED, i.e. someone who did the Chapel half-scene (§169 W21: Ros says only "She sent me", then "Not here... Later."). Records and Ward 9 both set MENDED, so the label names the wrong scene (in M22, Ward 9 is this very scene). Result: that reader is told "You know the rest" and never reads Ros's verbatim wake line anywhere in the book (CANON: told in full at the first MENDED scene). R41's second branch has the same hole. Fix: M22: delete the italic paragraph "*If you and Ros have already heard each other out...* Read on from *Ros laughs.*" and the word "*Otherwise:*"; keep Ros's "At the wake..." telling for everyone; make your own telling conditional: before "Then you tell her yours, because it's the only fair thing." insert "*If you have not told her already,*" and add before it "*If you told her in the chapel on the Old Wing,* she nods. 'I know yours. You said it by the candles. I said later. This is later.' Go on from *Ros laughs.*". R41: change "in Records or on Ward 9," to "in the chapel on the Old Wing,", and delete "*If she hasn't told you before,* she tells you now; if she has, she only says, 'You know the rest,' and you read on from *She turns her head.*" so it reads "'She sent me,' Ros says. Her voice is thick with sleep, and it's hers. 'At the wake. The Legion kitchen, at the sink, with her hat still on...'". Same one-phrase change in B09.
- [HIGH] §91 R11: "Something on your wrist lets go. A field is blank." — a reader can arrive with 0 fields filled (R08 and M09 allow for it; R11 does not), so the text is false and "Cross off 1 field" is impossible. Fix: replace "Something on your wrist lets go. A field is blank." with "*If at least one field of your Wristband is filled,* something on your wrist lets go. A field is blank. *Otherwise,* your wrist is bare, and the word goes through you anyway.", and replace "Cross off 1 field on your Wristband." with "*If at least one field of your Wristband is filled,* cross off 1 field on your Wristband. *Otherwise,* lose 2 Fear."

## MED

- [MED] §128 M17 (vs §196 R35): "'Let them go,' she says. 'That's it?" — Ros reads rule 8 aloud here. CANON says it is spoken aloud only at R35, and R35 claims "the first time anybody has said it in this hospital since 1979", which is false for anyone who took the M17 route. Fix: "She reads the card over your shoulder, her lips moving. 'That's it?' she says. 'That's the whole rule? Three words?'"
- [MED] §196 R35: "on the back, in biro, an M. and a rude little doodle" — only the M12 copy has the doodle (M20's back reads "Ward 9. 1999. Not yet. M."; M17's back is not described). False for two of three routes. Fix: "and on the back, in biro, Mum's M, the middle stroke dipping to the line."
- [MED] §38 R39: "You slide the ring off your little finger." — only B14 (§2) has you wear it on your little finger; B16 (§7) and LOCKER (§179) leave it in your hand or pocket. Fix: "You take the ring out. Her wedding band, worn thin as wire at the back by forty years of washing-up and dinner trays."
- [MED] §200 E_TRUE: "*If you have the RING,* Mum lifts her left hand" — R39 puts the ring on her finger on Ward 0 (RING_ON) but RING is never crossed off, so those readers "put the ring on" a second time. Fix: change the clause to "*If you have the RING and did not put it back on her finger on Ward 0,*" and add after the paragraph "*If you slipped the RING onto her finger on Ward 0,* it is still there when she lifts her left hand with her right, fingers spread. She looks at it. 'Where've you been?' she says to it."
- [MED] §96 M15 / §195 M16 / §128 M17: "nothing on the floor but green cotton and a paper mask" — after winning, the card is never picked up, yet M17 opens "out in the corridor with the card in your hand and the nappy pin still through one corner". M16 success ("the pin opens... the card slides into your hand") also sits oddly with "pin still through". Fix: M15: "...there's nothing on the floor but green cotton, a paper mask and a strip of card on its pink nappy pin. You snatch it up and go. Turn to {M17}." M16: "the pin opens with a tiny click and the card slides into your hand, the open pin still through its corner."
- [MED] §52 R10: "Some filled, some not, in her hand, the ink sunk into the plastic." — false for a reader with 0 fields (reachable via R08), as is "You've picked at them with a thumbnail all night" and "They don't come off for picking". Fix: before the field list add "*If any field of your Wristband is filled,*" and for the rest "*Otherwise,* your wrist is bare: NAME. DATE OF BIRTH. NEXT OF KIN. WARD. BED. All blank. 'Not yet, love,' says the Sister, without looking up. 'But we'll get there.'" and skip the "picked at them" and "Somebody's been at it" lines for that reader.
- [MED] §9 R26: "watch your sister take Mum's obs" — Ros is holding Mrs Price's Bay 4 chart (R25), Mum is behind the Bay 6 curtain you have not yet opened (R30 is the first sight of her). Fix: "You stand at her elbow with your hands in your pockets and watch your sister do the obs on Mrs Price, who holds out her wrist as if she'd been waiting all night to be asked, the way she means to do Mum's."
- [MED] §50 R19 and §110 R24: "PRICE, Edna. Admitted 21.3.79." — §72 R04 puts Mr A. Kerr in Bed 4 on the night of 22nd/23rd March 1979 (died 03.15), so Mrs Price cannot have been admitted to Bed 4 on the 21st. Fix: "Admitted 23.3.79." in both (and CANON line "4 Edna Price adm 21.3.79").
- [MED] §19 M13: "You don't know why she'd say that. Not yet." — false for a reader who has already been on Ward 9 (§87: Bed 3 by the window, your name on the board); M10 is reachable after Ward 9. Fix: "*If you have been on Ward 9,* you know exactly why. *Otherwise,* you don't know why she'd say that. Not yet."
- [MED] §144 R12: "a pair of surgical scissors, steel, blunt-nosed" — §16 W27 (CUT_W) describes them as "curved at the tips... finger-loops worn bright", and R12 repeats "Don't ask" as if new. Fix: "She's holding the surgical scissors, steel, curved at the tips, the finger-loops worn bright." and change "'Don't ask,' she says." to "'Don't,' she says."
- [MED] §112 R13: "Behind it, a mobile phone is ringing. A modern phone, the stock ringtone" — stated for every reader, but only a WARDED Ros is missing (R01 gates the ringing on WARDED; CANON: Bay 2 "R." only if WARDED). With Ros beside you the phone and the "R." card have no explanation and she does not react. Fix: put the ringing phone under "*If you have the codeword WARDED,*" and for the others "*If Ros is with you,* she pats her pocket. Her phone is there, silent. 'That's my ringtone,' she says. 'That's not mine.'" (card "R." stays).
- [MED] §193 E_ADMITTED: "*If your Wristband is full,*" — the three opening clauses (full band, last ROUNDS box, Fear limit) do not cover the main arrival, R31 "let her tuck you in" at four fields; also the section prints THE END twice (explicit "THE END" in the text plus the automatic one after the italic instruction). Fix: add "*If you let her tuck you in at the end of Ward 0,* you have said yes to what your body said long ago." as a fourth clause, and delete the explicit "THE END" line so the italic line "To begin the night again..." is followed by the automatic one only.
- [MED] §83 M12 and §128 M17 (and M09 §102): "Flat against the back panel, held there by nothing, is a strip of laminated card." — a reader who already holds THE LAST RULE (M14 or M20 first) finds a second copy with no acknowledgement, and Mum's "the bit I tore off" implies one strip. M10 and M14 are offered with no "lacks THE LAST RULE" gate. Fix: add to M12 and M17 an opening "*If you already have THE LAST RULE,* this is a second strip, the same type, the same ragged top, as if the hospital had printed her mistake more than once. Do not note it again. Turn to {M01}." (M12 can keep the doodle for the laugh; skip the Note.)
- [MED] §87 M18: no way out of Ward 9 — the choices are chair (M19), bed (D07), walk (M23) or parents' room (M25); M23 returns to M18 and the only exit to M01 is from M19/M21 after sitting in the chair. Fix: add "If you leave Ward 9, turn to {M01}." (STATE currently has no 87 -> 171 link).
- [MED] §49 R32: "Whichever you choose, cross the TABARD off your Adventure Sheet." — if you "keep it on" you turn to E_NIGHTSTAFF, which says "You keep it on". Fix: delete that sentence and write "If you take the tabard off, unpopping it down the side and folding it over the chair, cross the TABARD off your Adventure Sheet and turn to {R33}." (the sheet change then applies only to that link.)

## LOW

- [LOW] §19 M13: "The tenth key sticks." — §61 M11 says the outer lock opens on the seventh key and the inner lock has a dozen small keys; §83 M12 succeeds on "the ninth small key". Counting is inconsistent. Fix: "One of the small brass keys sticks."
- [LOW] §121 M27: "five floors down" — Maternity is Level 5, the Old Wing swing doors are off the Main Corridor on Level 1 (CANON), four floors down. Fix: "four floors down".
- [LOW] §171 M01: "nearly weren't eight behind the other" — you were already eight on Ward 9. Fix: "You were born behind one set of doors and, aged eight, very nearly stopped behind the other."
- [LOW] §176 R06a: "Not to the lift: to a door beside it" — the lift is back at the far end of the corridor, not just inside the Ward 0 doors; and "At the station the Sister turns a page" conflicts with R04 "The Sister has gone down the ward". Fix: "Not back towards the lift: to a door in the wall by the bench that you hadn't seen" and "At the station the Sister caps her pen and walks off down the ward."
- [LOW] §41 E_LOOP: "Turn to section 1 and begin again." followed by the automatic THE END. Fix: leave as is only if the engine's ending footer is switched off for E_LOOP; otherwise drop the footer for this ending.
- [LOW] §45 R23 and §158 R22: R23 says "step away from the bed" where every other chart section says "turn back"; R22 says at the third fold "One more fold, and she will be at the bed" but nothing happens at a fourth fold except 2 Fear. Fix: R23 "If something is wrong, and you turn back, turn to {R24}."; R22 "If the third time or more... the warm red light is on your shoes, and the ward is running out of folds."
- [LOW] §123 R17: "Lose 1 Fear." — the GLASSES option at R16 can be taken again after each D10 return (R16 is the checkpoint), so Fear can be farmed. Fix: add "Only the first time you put them on" before "Lose 1 Fear.".
