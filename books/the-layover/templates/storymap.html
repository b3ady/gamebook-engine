How the book is built

    Its rules, as the reader learns them

      The Claim track

      Every time you give it your name, a letter appears on its sign. At letters, turn to Final Call. Claim is driven by choices, not dice, so it works in Story Mode too.

      The lore trail

      Fourteen fragments. Each one opens a question or answers an earlier one, and many double as survival tips. Several sit on mutually exclusive branches, so no single read-through finds them all.

      Items & codewords

      Every key item can be found by several routes, so one bad roll never locks the reader out. Codewords record choices that pay off later.

      Deaths and their warnings

      Each death is foreshadowed by an earlier section and sends the reader back to a checkpoint. Damage is gradual elsewhere: Vigour and Fear wear the reader down instead.
