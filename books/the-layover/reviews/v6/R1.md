## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. HOURS is a clock with no teeth.**
*Where:* 16 has one threshold (5+ hours, +1 Claim), and both paths tick exactly 4. 61's passenger keys on items, not hours. 16 puts your hour count on the board, then 111, 172, 65 and 11 hard-code 05:10 to 05:47, so a reader with 3 ticks sees 3:00, then 05:10 a page later. Fear changes 8 times on P1 and 13 on P6, and gates nothing.
*Fix:* key 61's passenger blocks on hours. Set thresholds at 3 (passenger halfway, ledger line *05 · TIME ON GROUND*) and 5 (+Claim). Remove the numbers from the Gate 0 clocks. Let Fear 6+ lock out the eyes-shut walks at 16.

**2. The hub is renumbered, not split.**
*Where:* 61 ×5 on both paths. Every visit, the reader skips a 150-word first-visit block and parses four more conditionals. P6's hotel is 16 sections. 199, 134 and 195 have no repeat stub, so they reprint in full. 15 bounces back to 196.
*Fix:* give 61 a separate 40-word repeat section. Stub 199, 134 and 195. End 15 with its own choices. Send 100 to 134, not 47.

**3. The kind arc is spent at 45%.**
*Where:* P1 lands TOLD (163) and TRUCE (164) four sections apart, around section 40 of 88. After that, Sam gets about ten "*If you have TRUCE*" grace notes and has nothing left at stake. The ledger takes nothing from P1. The climax is a single Nerve test (111).
*Fix:* fire 160 on the second Atrium return, not the first, so it can't land next to 163. Or keep TRUCE provisional until the bridge. Give P1 a debt it has to pay: 148's 23:58 toastie is a minute from Rule 4, so make it 00:00 or ambiguous.

**4. Too many Sam codewords, and the ledger is still accounting.**
*Where:* THAW is still live (122, 116, 7, 57, 144). P6's climax runs 89 → 30 → 193 → 180 → 30 → 19 → 30 → 17 → 45 → 20 → 30 → 65. 17 → 45 → 20 is good action; the four 30s are paperwork. After 65, P6 runs P1's 14 sections (11 → 115).
*Fix:* fold THAW into CARE and recode 46. Resolve FED, CHECKED IN and MARKED from one ledger section with sub-options, which cuts 30 to two prints.

**5. False leads and a theme seam.**
*Where:* Capt. R, the "Captain" line in 100, 1987 ×3 and the 137 count all point somewhere 200 doesn't go. 200's thesis is "*Don't you two turn round*", but Rule 7 says turn back, and the bridge (44 → 181 → 69) rewards turning back.
*Fix:* cut Capt. R or make him the '98 pilot. Pay 1987 in 50's attachment, or cut two plants. Make 69 where turning stops working: the last move must be a step forward.
