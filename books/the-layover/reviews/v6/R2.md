## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. 16 is a Claim cliff placed before the reckoning.**
A reader at Claim 3 with 5 hours ticked reaches 16 and goes to 4 before choosing anything. Then 42 (no test, no warning) kills, and eyes-on kills. 193's forgiveness is two sections too late. Run 5 at 4 or 5 is one die roll from death at 42.
*Fix:* move "If your Claim is 3 or more and you do not have SIGNED" to the top of 16, before the hours tax. Or let the hours tax apply only at Claim 2 or less. Put "(a death: add 1 to your Claim)" on 42's choice line.

**2. The TRUCE reckoning is a Fear trap.**
Run 5's Fear (Story Mode, limit 12) is 8–9 after 64. TRUCE at 16 adds 2, giving 10–11, and the forced TRUCE line at 193 adds 1, giving **11–12**. If run 5 had walked blind at 64, its best bond would have killed it at the ledger. CARE gets −2 Claim with no Fear. A Survival reader with Nerve 7 (limit 10) is already dead at 16.
*Fix:* make TRUCE give −2 Claim and no Fear. Keep the Fear cost on the CARE branch, or on 17's MARKED fallout.

**3. HOURS contradicts the Gate 0 clock.**
At 16 the board shows the ticked hour followed by zeros. All three runs show 4:00. One walk later it is 05:04 at 37 or 05:10 at 111. A three-tick reader jumps two hours. A six-tick reader sees 6:00, past the 05:59 departure, and then boards at 05:47. Nothing says what happens after the sixth box is ticked. The 5-hour tax is announced nowhere until it is charged.
*Fix:* key Gate 0 times to the hours ticked ("ticked hour + :04"). Add "If all six boxes are ticked, turn to Final Call" to the hours rule. Have FRONT_RULES hint that late is costly.

**4. Two loops are still printed in full on every visit.**
61 is printed ×5 per run with five If-blocks. Its first-visit block always repeats 144 (escalators, galleries, the board line). 30 is reprinted after every debt. Three-way return lines appear at 84, 88 and 183.
*Fix:* fire RETURNED and HEARD from 61's top, and have every exit say only "Turn to 61". Cut the 144 duplicate. Print the ledger lines as tick-boxes on the Adventure Sheet.

**5. Fortune is still a one-shot.**
Each run tests Fortune once. The stat drops by 1 per test, so it never runs low. In Story Mode the reader simply picks Lucky at 156, so the second bridge never appears.
*Fix:* in Story Mode, make 156 alternate layouts on each retry instead of offering a choice. Move one of the Nerve tests (39, 45 or 111) to Fortune so the stat runs down across the night.
