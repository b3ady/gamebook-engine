## 3. Top 5 problems now

1. **Claim still ends up in the same place.** The reckless run had Claim 3 or more, two deaths and a name read aloud, and got the same stamp, seats, phone call and bridge as the careful one. The broken stamp is good flavour, but the only real cost is the secret lock. At Claim 4+, let the bridge or the thing at 69 change: one more face in the coat, or your own face.
2. **Gate 0 is paperwork.** Twelve sections of queue, scan, ledger, ledger again, stamp. "Settle the lines from the top" reads like a tax return. Merge 89/30/65.
3. **Hub fatigue.** 61 prints six times, and Sam says *"Hollowmouth's on time"* on every visit, which also contradicts GATE CLOSED at 144. The 88 fountain is the same problem, smaller.
4. **Characters explain the rules.** 4, 119, 193 and 65 hand you rules you could have worked out. 104 explains nothing and it's terrifying.
5. **The secret and its loose ends.** There are still four clues and the guard line at 137. The new last line, *"LV 1987. DELAYED."*, echoes the 1987 receipt but means nothing I can find. After a perfect goodbye it's a cheap sequel sting.

## 4. Scariest moments, ranked

1. **104, the bridge with nothing wrong.** *"There must be something, because there has always been something, and if you can't find it, it is somewhere you aren't looking."* That's Exit 8 turned against you.
2. **177/63, the hospital bed.** *"Its chest rises. The monitor beeps. Its chest falls. The two are not keeping time."* Then a shoulder turns under the sheet at the foot of the bed.
3. **35, the silent aircraft.** *"You can hear the joins tick, one by one, the way you never could before."*
4. **197, the lift.** The floor numbers go down to 0 and then come back up to 4. *"Something down there has called the lift."*
5. **25, the loop.** *"This time, you can read it."* Underneath your name, someone has started on Sam's.
6. **41, the break room.** *"The sound chairs make when the people in them start, slowly, to turn."*
7. **181, the faceless poster.** The smaller child's arm comes down, pointing at you.
8. **21.** Your reflection walks a half-step late, while Sam's keeps perfect time with him.
9. **42.** The felt pen *"squeaks again, and starts the next."*

## 5. Lore

**What hooks me:**
- **Dad and 1998.** Seat 1A, DID NOT BOARD (92). Six-year-old you crying on the phone (57). *"Turned round at a gate once"* (137), mirrored by your March turn-around (163). A family that keeps turning round at gates, never spelled out.
- **Walter:** 87 → 130 → 33 → 69 → 137 is still the best throughline.
- The child's hand on the sign (16), the child's capitals in the register (19) and the round, careful breath writing (139) all point at six-year-old you.

**What falls flat:**
- 1987. Kite was born around 1992, Dad in 1952. Whose date is 14/03/1987? Plant one more clue.
- Still no news clipping or CCTV log as found text. Neither run opened the newspapers at 52.
