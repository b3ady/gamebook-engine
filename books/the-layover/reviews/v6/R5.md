## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The confession owns up to the wrong lie.** At 18 the screen says *SEARCHING*, so "No signal" was true. The lie was "Nothing", said about a voicemail from Dad. 38 has the reader confess "I had signal", which is false. 200 has Sam throw back "No signal, you said", which was the honest half. *Fix:* in 38, "It wasn't nothing. At the gate. It was him. I didn't want to hear it." In 200, "'Nothing,' you said," Sam says.

**2. The hubs still talk over the story.** In route 6, 61 prints "Hollowmouth's on time. Good for Hollowmouth" six times. One comes straight after 14's "I didn't know how to stop meaning it", and he's back to quipping. The quip also contradicts 144 (*HOLLOWMOUTH. GATE CLOSED.*). 134's thumb-in-the-0 beat prints four times. 199's passport line prints twice. *Fix:* give 61's Sam clause and 134 a "*first visit only*" tag. Give 199 a revisit skip. In 134: "*If MARKED,* he uses the other thumb."

**3. Alone forgets what Sam said.** Route 2 gets 14 in full: "I meant it for about a year... So I kept typing the other thing." Then Alone (81) has nothing for it. Dad is still flawless there too: *LOVE YOU KITE*, and nothing else. *Fix:* in 81, add "*If you heard Sam say he stopped meaning it,* you start a text on the runway. *Go on, then.* You delete it." Move "Brave man. Scared of a phone call." into 95's TRUCE clause.

**4. FORGOT grows after the fact.** 180 takes one sound: "the way he said it". "You can say the word. It is a word you have read and never heard spoken." Then 38 says you "couldn't have said, now, how he sounded" (his whole voice), and 200 makes the tape "a stranger's voice, until it says *Kite*" (his whole voice again). *Fix:* in 200, "*If you have FORGOT,* it is his voice, all of it, until *Kite*, and then the word arrives like one you've only read." In 38, "you couldn't have said how he said your name."

**5. The "fine" payoff is spent before 160.** 190 explains it in advance: "*How's Dad? Fine... We're fine.*" Then 160 reveals the thread as if for the first time, and the reader says "You told me you were fine" on top of it. That is three printings, and the "x" lands on a worn-out motif. *Fix:* cut 190 down to "He has said *fine* to you a great many times." Let 160 show the actual words.
