## 3. Top 7 problems

**1. Neutral text that isn't neutral.**
- On path 4, Sam's phone is "dead" at 54 and then has three per cent at 150.
- For a CARE reader who charged it at 115, "The screen stays black" (59) is false.
- 135's "not close enough to touch" comes right after the THAW squeeze in 166.
- 185's "You put it on because she's holding it out" sits outside the no-uniform clause, and path 4 is already wearing one.
- The choice into 38 reads "pulling Sam with you if he's here", but the section is an unconditional Sam argument.

**2. Every reward is a touch.** Of 15 TRUCE/THAW blocks, 14 are contact: a sleeve ×4, a hand ×5, a shoulder ×3, a wrist, an arm. Twelve end in "doesn't let go / leaves it there / stays there". Only the original works: "Your elbow's in the wrong place. You leave it there." (17)

**3. Climaxes cut to stubs.**
- 92: "Sam walks round it like a pillar. You get up and go for it." That is the whole boss fight.
- 58: the Dad-thing's defeat gets four sentences.
- 182: "He's gone."
- 121: "The jet bridge."

**4. Frightened eyes.** The ending (185) depends on this image, and the book uses it five times first, including Dad's face in 30 and 163.

**5. Captions.**

**6. Muddled blocks.**
- 95: Sam "doesn't stop" reciting, then "stops at Saint Undermere". "'Nothing. We'll wait. Together.' He doesn't answer" puts two speakers in one paragraph.
- 111: "If you hold all three" is the only key-item line not set as a block.
- 163: a "Turn to" sits inside the LOST block.
- 185 and 38: conditionals are run inline, with no block.
- 14: "two things are left", then "one word left", which makes three.
- 59: the TRUCE line repeats "not queuing".
