## 1. Verdict: 8/10 (same as v3). Yes, I'd recommend it.

It's still scary, and the hubs finally change when you come back to them. But nothing big got fixed. Claim still gets wiped by a codeword, people still read you the rules, and my reckless run got through the ledger with "All debts settled." It keeps explaining itself.

## 3. Top 5 problems now

1. **Claim doesn't change anything.** It changes the lettering on the sign (94, 107, 194) and decides whether you visit 133. If you have TRUCE or CARE it costs you nothing. My reckless run died twice and still got the same stamp as my careful run. The ending came down to one choice at 30. High Claim should close a door: 0B doesn't print, or Sam's name is half gone from the pass.
2. **Everyone explains the rules.** That's the grey man, the Hostess three times (24, 160, 121), Walter, and Dad at 137. The book is scariest when nobody explains anything (108, 147). Cut 188's list and *"I don't make the rules."*
3. **The full-name tic.** It works as the opposite of *Kite* at 36 and 137. Ten times a run turns it into filler.
4. **Gate 0 is still slow.** It's ten sections of desk before the bridge, and if you fail on the bridge (90) you repeat the chant. P5 checked carpet, rail, windows, poster and hum five times.
5. **The motifs are used up.** By the third time you hear "many breaths" you know it means "it's here." The frightened-eyes payoff at 137 (*"His eyes are not frightened at all"*) is great, but it only lands if the book stops saying "frightened" every chapter.

## 4. Scariest moments, ranked

1. **147, the hospital bed.** *"Its chest rises. The monitor beeps. Its chest falls. The two are not keeping time."* Then *"Stay with your old dad, lad."* At 97 a shoulder turns under the sheet at the foot of the bed.
2. **105, the loop.** *"This time, you can read it."* Sam's name has been started underneath yours.
3. **108, the break room.** Staff sit facing the wall with skin on their tea, and in the dark the chairs creak *"the sound chairs make when the people in them start, slowly, to turn."* New to me, and perfect.
4. **40.** *"NO FAULT FOUND. The name goes on."*
5. **110.** The pen squeaks once, then *"starts the next."*
6. **30.** An old man with peppermint on his breath is pressed into the coat. If you promised Walter, that's him. That's a gut-punch.
7. **194.** The lift holds at 0, then climbs back up.
8. **126.** *"Every mouth is saying mum."*

## 5. Lore

**What hooks me:**
- **The voicemail.** *"Voicemail: Dad. 05:59"*, dated tomorrow, says *"I should have told you both why I never…"* and ties to ledger line 04, *"UNHEARD… Not at this desk."* Neither of my runs ever played it. Hidden branch or loose thread? I want to know.
- **The receipt says 14/03/1987, and the last line says LV 1987. DELAYED.** That's a sequel hook hidden in a toastie receipt. I love it.
- **Breath writing at 7:** *"BEHIND THE WHISKY"* in *"round and careful"* capitals. Is it the child's hand again? Is it yours? I'd make a theory video.
- **Walter's arc:** 165 → 138 → 30 → 137. It's the best throughline in the book.

**What falls flat:**
- 137's guard line (*"you have turned to the wrong page"*) admits the secret is just "turn to the flight number." Four clues for a number printed in every header is too many.
- Dad gives a speech about 1998. He should show it, not say it.
- Still no CCTV log or clipping. The receipt and voicemail prove you can write found text, so do more.

## 6. Boredom points

- 111 and 171 still come up five times a run. The skip lines help, but I'm still turning pages to choose the same way.
- 33 is printed twice per run.
- 143 comes back in full after the PA death.
- The jet bridge chant (see Problem 4).
- *"If Sam is with you"* appears in nearly every section, and he was with me in both runs.

## 8. What works

- **The hub changes** (coins, apples, planter, Food Court light). Spotting them is exactly the Exit 8 itch.
- **Sam.** *"Breathe when I do"* (147), *"He told me first. Made me swear not to ring you."* He's the reason the book hurts.
- **The Kite tell** at 36 is still the best rule in the book, because it's emotional and not printed in the Handbook.
- **The 58 → 105 loop.** It's still the best bad ending I've read in a gamebook.
- **Walter.** The promise pays off three separate times.
