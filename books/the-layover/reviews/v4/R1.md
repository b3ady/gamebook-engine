## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. Hubs are still the book's main weight.**
*Where:* 3,336 words reprinted on P6 and 2,238 on P1. The hotel stretch on P6 is 16 sections (140 → … → 195) with three lift trips and two bell rings.
*Why:* every reprint resets dread.
*Fix:* merge 111 into 171. Make return visits separate 30-word sections, or add tick boxes to the sheet. Cut 116 → 54 so 116 exits to 21/66.

**2. P6's climax is a toll queue.**
*Where:* 160 → 33 → 133 → 29 → 33 → 25 → 33 → 86 → 16 → 33 → 20. That's 11 sections and one real choice. 86's alternative needs TRUCE, so P6 can only drag.
*Why:* the best new scene is buried between ledger reprints. The reader is processed, not tested.
*Fix:* print the ledger once and give each line its own section. Give 25 a refusal that costs clock time ("*05:5x*") or Vigour. Move the MARKED page (86/16) before 160, so the desk only does accounting.

**3. Grace is still free, and the new cost isn't carried through.**
*Where:* at 133, TRUCE or CARE erases 2 letters for nothing. In 114, a reader with FORGOT never notices the loss, even though they're holding the voicemail.
*Why:* 29 shows what a price looks like, and 133 gives the discount anyway.
*Fix:* make Sam pay at 133. He gives up one of his cities (Port Ever goes blank on the board), and 200/58 mention it. Add a FORGOT line to 114: "*The preview says Kite. You read it like a stranger's word.*"

**4. Sam is tracked by four codewords.**
*Where:* on P1 he touches you about eight times before 115 (164, 141 ×2, 166, 42, 156, 17, 10). 17 accepts THAW in place of TRUCE. 150/115 ignore TOLD.
*Why:* 115's "*You should have come*", after "*I got as far as the gate*", reads like a continuity error.
*Fix:* fold THAW into TRUCE. Cap touches at three before 115. Write a TOLD variant of 150 that starts from March, not from the airport.

**5. Unpaid lore and broken rules.**
*Where:* the seat line at 0A/0B vs 114; "*Eyes shut*"; 1987 "*SEE ATTACHED*"; three 137 counts; the UNIFORM gate.
*Fix:* in 114, Sam sits in 0B facing the aisle. Change "*Eyes shut*" to "*Eyes open*". Either attach 1987 (Dad's first turn-back) or redate it. Keep 50's count and cut the other two.
