## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The Claim reckoning comes after the Claim cliff.**
133 forgives 2 letters for everyone, but only at 33. Every heavy cost (163, 85, 110, 94) falls before it. In 3b, Claim is 4 entering the second 94. Run 5 is also at 4 after 110, and failing at 9 or watching at 85 puts it at 5 inside 110. A LOST reader's survival depends on one Nerve test at 172, where failure means Final Call.
*Fix:* make 110 cost 1. Make 172's failure cost +2 Fear and send the reader back to 94, not to a death. Let 49 (*"You came back for me"*) take off 1 Claim. Print *"If your Claim is now 5, turn to Final Call"* inside 110.

**2. The bridge is still a memory test, and 121 now states the wrong rule.**
*"Three clean walks and it lets you through"*, but the solution is one clean walk and two wrong ones. A reader who follows the Hostess turns back at 34 and fails.
*Fix:* reword it as *"Three walks. If nothing is wrong, walk on. If something is, turn back."* On a retry, test Fortune to choose from a pool of anomalies.

**3. Two hubs, five reprints.**
111 and 171 both work as Atrium menus. Returns land in either one depending on where you came from. 111→178→171 establishes the Atrium three times in a row. 33 is still printed in full four times in run 4.
*Fix:* make 111 the only hub. Turn 171 into a stub that says "the Atrium; choose from 111". Move the ledger lines onto the Adventure Sheet as a checklist.

**4. Claim taxes the rules never mention.**
FRONT_RULES says Claim rises when you give your name or die. In practice it also rises when you look (187, 163, 85, 94, 194) and when you fail a chase (9). Run 5 survives only if it passes 9, passes 77 and shuts its eyes at 85. Any other combination gives Claim 5 at 110 or Fear 12 at 94.
*Fix:* add one rules sentence: *"…and every time it learns your face."* Put each cost on the choice line it belongs to.

**5. Fortune and THAW are still ornaments.**
There is one Fortune test per route, and Story Mode removes even that one. THAW buys a hand-squeeze.
*Fix:* turn the Nerve tests at 9 and 77 into Fortune tests. Let THAW count as CARE at 94 and 133, which makes THAW → CARE → TRUCE a real ladder.

## 5. Mechanics notes

- **Claim (hand-counted; the generator does not track it):** 3b is at 4 before the second 94 and 5 as printed. Run 5 is at 4 before 133, then 2. Run 4 is at least 2 (159, 119, plus whichever choices it made at 194 and 85) and never hits 133. 105 carries Claim into a new loop, so a reader who loops at Claim 4 starts the night almost dead. The generator took 133 in run 5 but skipped it in 3b.
- **Fear:** Run 5 peaks at 11 at 94 (TRUCE blind +2) and again at 90. The Story Mode limit is 12, and a failure at 77 makes it 12. That is the right pressure for a reckless run. Survival readers with Nerve 7–8 die there. The rescue route peaks around 8–9, so Fear still barely matters on it.
- **Checkpoints:** Unchanged. A Vigour death usually rewinds one to three sections, and 110 still rewinds exactly one.
- **Rules:** *"Codewords are exact"* is good. "Key item" (111) is never defined.
- **Fights/chases:** 92 (Announcer, Nerve 9, Vigour 12) is fair. 9's +1 Claim on a failure is still the hidden tax behind #4.
