## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The reckoning turns Claim upside down.**
165 crosses off 2 letters at *exactly* 3 and nothing at 4. A reader at 2 or less never reaches it. So 3 ends at 1, which is lower than 2. Run 5 reaches 16 at Claim 2 and dies at 42, and the reckoning hands it back at 1: the death was a profit. In 3b, 42 plus eyes-on gives 4, 142 gives 3 and 165 gives **1**, so the reader who lost Sam ends cleaner than one who kept him. Below 4, "stake a letter" is a bluff.
*Fix:* reduce by steps for any Claim of 2 or more, 4 included: TRUCE −2, CARE −1, 14 −1. A higher Claim must always end worse.

**2. The Claim-4 lockout is hidden, and the reader plays on without knowing it.**
FRONT_RULES names 5 as the limit. 16's warning, "one more letter and it has you", implies 4 is safe. 30's "Sign your real name… add 1 to your Claim" turns 3 into 4 with no cost on the line. After 165 says "Nothing is crossed off", the reader plays about 12 more sections (the stamp, Walter, the bridge's folds and Fortune test, "Goodbye, Dad"). Only then does 120 eject them to Ground Crew.
*Fix:* add one FRONT_RULES sentence: "At 4, no seat will take you." Put the cost on 30's sign option. At 165's Claim-4 block, offer a priced way out, such as 14 taking Kite and PROMISE.

**3. The loop soft-locks a reader at Claim 4.**
81 says "keep your Claim". The only reductions in the book are 142 (LOST only), 165 and 14 (at exactly 3). A reader who loops at 4 replays 90 sections with the true ending shut. Every letter is death, and the only key is to lose Sam on purpose.
*Fix:* 81 sets Claim to 2 if it is higher ("the night forgets some of you").

**4. The finale's Fear margin is one mistake.**
Run 5 in Story Mode reaches 44 at about 8–9 Fear, after the TRUCE crossing and 82's −2. The fold makes it 10–11 against a limit of 12. One more wrong turn on the bridge is Final Call at the door of the true ending. A Survival reader with Nerve 7 has a limit of 10 and is already dead at 44. Story readers will also pick "Lucky" at 44, which sends them back to 168 to guess again.
*Fix:* the first fold costs 2 Fear and later folds cost 1. Add a note to Story Mode: "Unlucky is not always worse."

**5. 37/106 repeats the 42 mistake.**
"If you run straight through the door into Gate 00" names no cost, but 106 is a death. 39's failed Nerve test also lands there. 3b has just been pushed to 4, so its LOST rescue rests on one roll (about 58% at Nerve 7) between Arrival and Final Call.
*Fix:* add "(it costs a letter)" to the line. A failed 39 should give +2 Fear and a retry instead.
