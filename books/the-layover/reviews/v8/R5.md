## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The kind route's TRUCE loss is a dice roll that the ending ignores.** At 104 the reader has already chosen to wait with Sam ("let him be the one"). A failed Nerve test then makes them do the opposite (21: "You can't... You take his wrist"). The worst moment between the siblings is taken out of the reader's hands. It then costs almost nothing. A TOLD reader goes 135 → 181 → 156 → 120. There Sam jokes about Dad's coat as if the bridge never happened. In 200 he still gives up the window ("You never get it") and still says "Safe landings, Kite", because nothing in 200 is gated. 21's last line also explains itself. *Fix:* make 104 a real choice. "Take his wrist" goes to 21, and "stand with him" goes to a Nerve test that only costs Fear. Cut 21's last line, because "three-years-of-*fine* voice" has already done the work. In 200, gate the window: "*If you have TRUCE,* Sam gave you the window..." Everyone else gets: "Sam kept the window. Somewhere over the sea he took one earbud out and held it across without looking." Gate "Safe landings, Kite" on TRUCE as well. Without it, he says "Come on. We'll be late," and bumps your knee once.

**2. Sam makes his case three times on route 6.** 160 ("I liked being the one he got"), 95 ("You never let me grieve... You did the funeral flowers by email") and 69 ("I did the tablets. I did the nights") are the same accusation from the same man within about forty sections. By 69 the reader has heard it twice, so the climax arrives already spent. *Fix:* cut 95 down to the minute he asks for: "I know it's not him. I was holding his hand when he—" then "I want one minute with it. One minute." Keep the caretaking list for 69 only.

**3. The Alone ending tells you Dad's flaw instead of showing it.** 78: "He was good at not telling. He kept the diagnosis from you for four months, so you wouldn't worry. Sam knew the whole time." That nearly repeats 144 word for word ("four months after the scan... Sam had known the whole time"), and the narrator delivers it as a verdict. *Fix:* "*If you told Sam he never asked you to...* before the descent you start a text to him. *How long did you mean it for?* You delete it." Stop there, because the reader remembers 147. For Dad, use an image instead of a summary: "...*why I never…* He had four months to tell you about the scan, and he talked about the weather." Move "Brave man. Scared of a phone call." into 95's main text, so cold routes hear it from Sam.

**4. Dad still states the moral.** This is the fourth review to flag it. 200: "turned round" twice, then "Don't you two turn round." *Fix:* "...one of you was crying down it. So I turned round. For you two." Stop there.

**5. Doubles that keep surviving.** Walter: the phone call at 82 blunts Ruth at 169. *Fix:* cut 82's phone call and let 169 carry it. Impostor: in 181, cut "and says your full name". Then "kiddo" or "sweetheart" is the only tell, and it's a better one.
