## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The rescue route is fatal: 3b hits Final Call at the second 94.**
Count: 159 VOUCHER +1, 163 at the window +1, 110 +2, then back at 94 the LOST reader has no TRUCE (Sam is gone) and no INCIDENT, so the only exit, eyes on it, adds +1. That is **Claim 5**, before 107, at the very least. Failing the Nerve test at 9, or watching the doorway at 85, gets the reader there sooner. Run 5 sits at 4 after 110 and is one bad roll at 9 from the same fate.
*Fix:* make 110 cost 1. Give the LOST reader a third option at 94 (say rule one aloud and hold Sam's passport: +2 Fear). Print *"If your Claim is now 5, turn to Final Call"* inside 110.

**2. TRUCE is a master key, and THAW is decoration.**
TRUCE now gates 29 (−2 Claim), the 94 blind walk, 69 (Sam stays), 163→17 and 16. One conversation (150) or one Nerve test (170) decides the Claim economy. THAW gates only 163, and otherwise changes a hand-hold.
*Fix:* let 29 also accept PROMISE (*"Walter's girl will remember your name"*) or a Fear price. Fold THAW into a ladder: THAW + 150 = TRUCE.

**3. The bridge is still a memory test.**
The same three anomalies come back on every retry, and Sam narrates the answer. 30's failure costs only 2 Fear.
*Fix:* on a retry, test Fortune to pick from a small pool of anomalies. Cut Sam's line at 90. A third failure costs 1 Claim.

**4. Hub and ledger repetition.**
111 and 171 are separate hubs, and returns from Duty-Free (37→111) and Lost & Found (177→171) land in different ones. 33 is printed four times in run 4. 118 offers *"search the hotel again for another way to him"*, but no such way exists. It's a dead loop at 05:04.
*Fix:* one hub, with 171 as a revisit stub that says "turn to 111's choices". Turn the ledger into a checklist on the Adventure Sheet. Cut 118→171, or give it real content (Sam's box at 50).

**5. Fortune is vestigial.**
One test each in 3b (63) and run 4 (13), none in run 5.
*Fix:* replace two Nerve tests (9, 77) with Fortune tests.

## 5. Mechanics notes

- **Claim:** Strong. The sign and ledger read it, 29 reckons it, 105 cites it. But the cap and the costs are tuned for readers who have Sam. The generator must track it.
- **Fear:** The rescue route peaks at about 7 and barely matters. Run 5 reaches 9–11, which is Final Call at Survival Nerve 7. 85's *"Gain 1 Fear… gain 1 more"* is ambiguous for the watcher: say which option the base cost applies to.
- **Deaths and checkpoints:** Now legitimate. Thin them out so that a Vigour death (the rule's "last ✈") actually costs something.
- **Story Mode:** Works; choosing Unlucky at 63 opens LOST honestly.
- **Ledger:** 33 still loops verbatim.
- **Chases:** 9 and 77 follow the stated rule. 9's +1 Claim on a failure makes it a hidden Claim tax.
- **Hub escalation:** Good. The planters, coins, apples and food court creep forward, and the passenger's position tracks your item count. The clock is still fake.
- **THAW/TRUCE/CARE:** See #2. CARE is the only backup at 29 and never appears on these routes.

## 6. What works

- 29: your name in felt pen in the ledger, read away *"together."* The best payoff in the book.
- 49: *"You came back for me,"* crossing off LOST and granting TRUCE. The rescue really does reset the relationship.
- 94: three exits priced in Fear, Claim and lore (INCIDENT), with the cost spelled out.
- 160→33 line 04 →200: a debt only the epilogue can settle.
