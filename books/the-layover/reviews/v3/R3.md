## 3. Top 7 language problems now

**1. Broken conditional blocks.** In seven places, text that belongs inside a block sits outside it, so readers get lines meant for someone else:
- 166: the glaring paragraph follows the THAW squeeze.
- 81: "'You always want window.' / 'Yeah… I know.'" reaches non-TRUCE readers.
- 22: "He laughs, and the laugh runs away down the glass" reaches non-TRUCE readers.
- 39: "Gain 2 Vigour" sits under the *LOST:* header.
- 69: a TOLD reader reads both confessions.
- 105: "tells you why" is followed by anything from zero to three reasons.
- 200: the FORGOT block says "Then he says *Kite*" after Dad has said it twice.

**2. LOST lines are still everywhere.** There are 40, and every reader with Sam beside them reads them all. Some are padding: "and wait, in case that counts" (37).

**3. Quota substitutes.**
- Hands in pockets became the dead phone (8).
- "A pace ahead" became measured distances: "a carpet's width", "one window-width", "ten paces back" (9).
- Speaking to objects became Sam reading things (14), with "reads it upside down" twice (91, 160).
- "Level" became "flat" (5), plus the same carer-on-the-phone gloss three times (74, 178, 16).
- "Not well. Perfectly." became "It fits." ×4.

**4. "At once."** It appears 20 times. The board turns over "at once" in six of them.

**5. Captions in new places.** These lines explain what the scene already shows:
- "So it shows things as they are. You aren't sure that's a gift." (177)
- "…you have had a whole night to learn what that tends to mean here." (34)
- "The airport, you think, is waiting to see whether you're the kind of people who stop." (190)
- "The only thing missing is the thing with his name in it." (179)
- "You can't feel anything about it." (105)

**6. Frightened eyes.** The image is still in 6 scenes (18, 107, 28, 163, 14, 105), and Dad gets it three times. "Every tooth in its place. / Her eyes are terrified." survives verbatim. The ending needs that image fresh, and the book spends it five times first.

**7. Repetition and over-trimming.**
- Hub 111 has no "been here before" version, so its 330 words repeat four times per path.
- Ledger 33 is printed four times, unchanged, while claiming "one more line is ruled through".
- The bridge inventory is recited five times.
- At the other extreme, the monster's defeat gets four sentences (75), and other beats are clipped: "He's gone." (86), "You get up and go for it." (20), "The jet bridge." (65).
