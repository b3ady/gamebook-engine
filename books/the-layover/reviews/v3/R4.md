# R4 (v3): Horror fan review of THE LAYOVER

## 1. Verdict: 8/10 (up from 7.5). Yes, I'd recommend it.

The fights are gone, the dark follows you one gate behind, and the bad ending is the best page in the book. Held back by the full-name tic, Gate 0 admin, and characters who read you the wiki. Claim has a gate now, but one codeword switches it off.

## 3. Top 5 problems now

1. **The full-name tic.** Chilling at 167; filler by 25. Keep it for 167, 40 and 36 only.
2. **Gate 0 is paperwork.** 107 → 59 → 24 → 160 → 33 → 25 → 182 → 33 → 138 → 174 → 81 → 65. Twelve sections of ledgers and stamps, and 33 reprints the whole account each time.
3. **Claim can be undone too easily.** In reckless P5, TRUCE erased the damage at 29 and the ending came down to one choice at 14. Deaths rewind to verbatim text. 67 shows the fix; do it on every return.
4. **Characters read you the wiki.** The grey man lists the items (188). The Hostess explains the mechanic: *"You get out on your own names. 0A. 0B."* (160). Dad explains 1998 (137). Explanation kills dread.
5. **The motifs are wearing out.** *"Many breaths, out of time"* comes back in 141, 184, 85, 94 and 30. *"Shoulders at different heights"* and *"a child's hand"* are repeated in 187, 194, 111 and 94. Reveals turned into checklists.

## 4. Scariest moments, ranked

1. **67, the loop.** *"This time, you can read it. It's your name... And underneath, smaller, someone has started on Sam's."* This is how death should feel.
2. **147, the hospital bed.** *"Its chest rises. The monitor beeps. Its chest falls. The two are not keeping time."* Then: *"You didn't, last time."*
3. **40, the PA.** *"From your chest. From behind your teeth."* → *"NO FAULT FOUND. The name goes on."*
4. **110, the pen.** *"a felt pen squeaks on card, finishing a letter. Then it squeaks again, and starts the next."* The second squeak is the scary part.
5. **58.** *"the hum you've heard all night was never an aircraft."* Retroactive horror.
6. **194, the lift numbers.** It holds at 0, then climbs again, and the doors open on *"folded to fit."*
7. **126.** The child's face *"opens from the hairline down... every mouth is saying mum."*

## 5. Lore

**What hooks me:**
- **You were six in 1998.** The crayon plane *"in your six-year-old hand"* (50), the radio *"from the year you were six"* (80), the phone memory (42), the *"child's careful capitals"* (182). Is the child's hand on the sign yours? Never confirmed. Theory video.
- **The ledger's line 04:** *"1 MSG · 05:59 · UNHEARD... Not at this desk."*
- **Walter's phone call** (174): *"She always made a sign."* It mirrors the entity's sign.
- **Room 1952** and the Night Manager's ring. Untold, good.
- **LV 1987. DELAYED.**

**What falls flat:**
- 160 and 137 explain the rules out loud.
- The secret is over-clued: "transfer"/"number" on the tag, packet, last page and pass, and the answer is the flight number printed on every page.
- There's still no CCTV log, clipping or PA transcript.

## 6. Boredom points

- The jet bridge chant, *"Carpet, rail, windows, poster, hum."* It comes up at 65, 34, 112 and 90, and P5's fold at 30 made me reread 34 word for word.

## 8. What works

- **The chases.** The suitcase thing, now fled not fought; the card that *"isn't blank any more"* is a great cost.
- **85.** The red dot finally pays off, and watching costs you.
- **29.** *"It's been writing you in all night."* Claim bites.
- **The 58 → 67 loop ending.** It's the best page in the book.
- **Sam.** *"You never let me grieve"* (38), and *"He told me first. Made me swear not to ring you"* (147), stop him being a sidekick.
- **The Kite tell still lands:** *"It doesn't know that."*
