## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. Repetition is now the book's biggest weight.**
*Where:* 111 (320 words) always sits beside 171 (197 words). So 37 → 111 → 36 → 95 → 171 makes you cross the Atrium twice in one visit. P6 reprints 19 three times and 33 four times.
*Why:* every reprint resets dread. P6's hotel stretch (140 … 76) has 13 sections and three lift trips.
*Fix:* merge 111 into 171 (keyed on item count, routing to 178/36). Give return visits to 19, 140, 161 and 76 30-word stubs. Make 33 a single page with "*rule off each line you qualify for, then go to 138*".

**2. Claim still costs the reckless reader nothing.**
*Where:* P6 reaches Claim 4+ (159, 119, 88, 94) and is sent to 29. CARE erases two letters automatically. 182 then adds Claim *after* SIGNED, and nothing checks it again. 25 forces the signature: its only exits are "*return it and sign*" or the UNIFORM.
*Why:* signing your name to the thing is the book's central horror, and it has become a toll booth with a discount.
*Fix:* make the CARE/TRUCE erase cost something (Sam takes a letter: his initial appears). Recheck Claim at 138. Give 25 a refusal that costs time or Vigour.

**3. The true ending pays off an unplanted voicemail.**
*Where:* the voicemail is introduced only at 142 (phone your aunt). P1 takes 155 and never sees it. Then 200 says "*The missed call from a morning that hadn't happened yet*". The only hint is ledger line 04.
*Why:* on the kind route, the best scene arrives as exposition.
*Fix:* plant the notification in 35 or 123 for every reader. Keep 142's transcript line as the branch-specific detail.

**4. Sam's state is now four codewords and a costume.**
*Where:* THAW, TRUCE, TOLD and CARE, plus LOST. P1 has about 15 touches (141, 166, 42 … 75, 124). 150 also ignores TOLD: after 10's confession Sam still argues "*You took it like a ticket*" as if March were unsaid.
*Why:* the tracks overlap, so each one means less. Touch is wallpaper again.
*Fix:* fold THAW into a TRUCE 0–2 track. Cap touch at three before 115. Give 150 a TOLD variant.

**5. Rule breaks and dangling threads.**
*Where:* 160: "*You get out on your own names. 0A. 0B.*" Yet in 136 Sam sits "*Twenty rows*" back with no consequence. "*Eyes shut*" contradicts a bridge you pass by looking. 91: "*PREVIOUS IRREGULAR OPERATION: 1987. SEE ATTACHED*" is never paid off on either route, though the 1987 receipt opens the book (155, 116). The child's hand on the sign (94, 111, 182) is still unexplained.
*Fix:* Sam sits in 0B, back to you, earbuds in. Pay off 1987 in one line, or date the receipt 1998. Tie the child's hand to 149's "*you were crying down the phone*" explicitly.
