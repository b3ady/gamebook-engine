# R5 v3: Characters and Relationships

## 1. Verdict: 8/10 (v2: 7)

All five of my v2 problems were addressed, and four are actually fixed. Cold Sam is now a person in a mood rather than a stage direction, and "He didn't" is the second-best scene in the book. What holds it back now is wiring rather than writing: CARE rewards the wrong act, a gesture of Sam's is undone without anyone noticing, and the voicemail's secret is told before it plays.

## 3. Top 5 problems now

**1. CARE rewards the failure and ignores the rescue.** 176 ("Your hand doesn't move fast enough… 'Here,' Sam says") gives CARE. 166, where you actually stop him, gives nothing, and the only other source is F17. The reader learns that letting Sam get marked is the caring choice. *Fix:* 166 gives CARE, and so does success at 92 for readers who don't already have it.

**2. Sam crosses out your name, then you sign it again.** In route 6, at 29, Sam "draws a line through your name, slow and hard." Straight after, at 182, you sign the register in full, your Claim goes up by 1, and all Sam says is "You signed it." The book forgets his one gesture. *Fix:* add a CARE line to 182, "I just crossed that out." Or put 29 after the last ledger line, so the reckoning really comes last.

**3. The voicemail's reveal is told first.** In 42 Sam tells the '98 turn-back in full, and in 149 he uses it as an accusation. When Arrival plays "I turned round at a gate once, for you two", only the instruction is new. *Fix:* in 42, Sam knows only the fact: "Never flew again after '98. Never said why. I asked."

**4. The reader's first lie never comes due.** At 142 you tell Sam "Nothing. No signal." In the Separate Seats car park, "you watch him understand what you're asking", but you told this Sam there was no message. *Fix:* make that beat a confession: "It's from Dad. It came at the gate. I said it was nothing." Then "Not today." On the warm route, give Sam one line before you press play: "You said it was nothing."

**5. Arrival over-ends.** After the message come "I can't be the only one left" (which explains the line before it), the knee bump, the shoulder lean, and then "Safe landings, Kite." with no speaker. *Fix:* cut that sentence. Make "Safe landings" Dad's sign-off on the tape, and end on "Took you long enough."

## 4. Characters

**Sam.** He is the real achievement of this revision. His withheld warmth is specific and wry: "Rule four. I *know*," as he picks up a tray. In 133 he catches himself starting "Go on, then", which gives him a conscience without an apology. The flinch at 149 is exactly right. The dead phone appears 4 times in route 6, so drop one of them. If you plugged it in at 150, it shouldn't be dead at 59.

**The reader.** Plugging in his phone and saying nothing is the best thing the reader does: an act instead of a speech. But March gets confessed three ways (10, 69, 149), while tonight's lie never gets confessed.

**Kite.** "He says it the way Dad said it, and you'd forgotten he had the right to" (26). That one line justifies the whole naming device. Let Sam say "Kite" once on the warm route too.

**Dad.** The message finally gives him a voice of his own. On cold routes he is still a coat and a playlist. Move "Brave man. Scared of a phone call." to a shared section such as 190.

**Hostess.** "I don't make the rules. They make me." She has a spine now. Seed "Margaret" at 18.

**Endings vs routes.** Alone follows "Kite, please" well. Separate Seats follows "It doesn't cover March" well, and "I've done worse corridors" earns his twenty rows. In route 2, though, Alone ignores Sam owning his line at 133. Add one clause to 185 for readers who saw it. The two endings also share the "belt buckled across the cushion" image, so give it to one of them only.

**KID variants.** They are clean now. "Kiddo" sits naturally beside "sweetheart" and "love".
