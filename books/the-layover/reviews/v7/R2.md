## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. 42 is an unmarked death, and the Claim-4 warning hides on the retry.**
The Claim-4 block warns about "any way across that costs a letter", but 42's line names no cost. And 42 returns you to 16, whose "Go straight to the choices" skips every If-block, the warning included. The reader 42 just pushed to 4 never sees it.
*Fix:* put the cost on the line ("run for Gate 0 and trust the dark (it costs a letter): turn to 42"). Make the Claim-4 line the first choice-area line, below the skip point.

**2. The bond crossings at 16 are still the expensive ones.**
At 16, TRUCE costs 2 Fear, CARE 3, INCIDENT nothing. Run 5 arrives at Fear 8 (blind at 64); TRUCE makes 10. Fine in Story Mode, but a Survival reader with Nerve 7 dies for holding Sam's hand. The book's emotional spine is the most punishing way across.
*Fix:* TRUCE −0 or 1 Fear, CARE 2. Let the cost signal how good the bond is, not the reverse.

**3. 119 still talks to LOST readers as if Sam were present.**
"Leave Sam with us": 3b hears it after 185's "*Sam. Checked in.*"
*Fix:* add an If-block: "*If you have LOST,* … *He's already boarded. You may join him.*" 

**4. The hub and return plumbing is still printed in full every lap.**
61 (six If-blocks, first-visit text repeating 144) appears ×5 per run. The four-way return block is copied into 84, 88 and 183, while 64 ticks and skips it.
*Fix:* add one "Return to the Atrium" section that ticks, checks the sixth hour and routes 147/90/119/61. Every exit, 64 included, turns there.

**5. Fortune is still a one-shot, and Story Mode deletes the second bridge.**
One test per run. At 156 a Story reader picks Lucky and never meets 35/104/21, the best sequence in the book.
*Fix:* in Story Mode, 156 should alternate on each retry: first fold Unlucky, second fold Lucky. Move one Nerve test (39, 45 or 111) to Fortune so the stat drains.
