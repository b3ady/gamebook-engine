## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. Alone's new runway text tells readers things they never heard.** 81: "*How long did you mean it for?* You know the answer, or you could have had it for the asking: about a year." Route 2 heard 14, but a 164 reader never heard "about a year", and a LOST reader skips 160 entirely (90 sends LOST to 61), so "it" points at nothing. "Or you could have had it for the asking" is the author hedging, not the character thinking. Dad is still flawless here too. *Fix:* gate it. "*If you told Sam he never asked you,* you start a text..." For TRUCE: "*If you have TRUCE,* you start a text: *Window's yours next time.* You delete it." LOST readers get nothing. Move "Brave man. Scared of a phone call." from 57 into 95's main text so cold routes hear it.

**2. Section 11's hand-hold prints for everyone.** "Sam's hand finds yours... He holds on until the speakers click off." 117 has said he hasn't let you touch him in three years. A reader with only 170 ("Calm down") and 14 behind them, and no CARE or TRUCE, still gets this. Route 6 only gets away with it because of 20. *Fix:* "*If you have CARE or TRUCE,* Sam's hand finds yours..." Everyone else gets: "Sam goes through first, hands in his pockets." Then pay off the window in 38: "He has a window back there. He always has the window."

**3. 189 puts Sam's line in the reader's mouth.** The exchange runs: reader "You told me not to come back." / Sam "...You still didn't come." / then "You let me do it alone." By alternation that last line is the reader's, but only Sam can mean it. *Fix:* "'You let me do it alone,' he says."

**4. Dad still says the theme out loud.** 200: "So I turned round. I turned round at a gate once, for you two. Don't you two turn round." That is "turned round" twice in a row, then the moral. This is the third review to flag it. *Fix:* "...one of you was crying down it. So I turned round. For you two." Stop there and let 115 and 38 do the rest.

**5. Doubles that keep surviving.** Walter gets the phone at 33 and Ruth at 78, and the second blunts the first. The impostor gets two tells: 119 makes the full-name point, and 69 makes it again with a pet name added. *Fix:* cut 33's phone call and let Ruth carry it, or keep the phone and have Ruth only nod. In 69, cut "and says your full name". The pet name alone shows that it doesn't know *Kite*.
