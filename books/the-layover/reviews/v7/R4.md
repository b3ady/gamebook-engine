## 3. Top 5 problems now

1. **Claim has no teeth.** The reckless run built Claim 3+, got 2 letters back at 193 and reached the same desk, stamp and bridge as the careful run. Ink lifting off "in flakes" is a lovely image, but it's an undo button. Make the letters cost something you keep. Let one face in the coat at 69 be yours.
2. **Gate 0 is still a tax return.** That's eleven sections from the queue to the first step on the bridge, including the same ledger printed twice and "settle the lines from the top, taking the first that applies". Merge 89, 30 and 65 into a single page.
3. **The secret is over-clued, and its ending has no payoff.** 49 and 13 both give TRANSFER. Then the page and the pass repeat the same small print. Cut one. The "wrong page" guard at 137 breaks immersion. And "LV 1987" is a sting with nothing behind it.
4. **The book still explains its own tells.** Kite/full name is the best tell in the book. 119 explains it and 12 explains it again. Trust the reader. 104 explains nothing, and that's exactly why it works.
5. **Repeats, hubs and rewinds.** P3 printed 61 six times. P5 printed 70, 28, 88, 16 and 30 twice each. Both runs open 1 → 36 → 148 → 67 word for word.

## 4. Scariest moments, ranked

1. **104, nothing wrong.** *"There must be something, because there has always been something."* It's still the best page in the book.
2. **177/63, the hospital bed.** The chest and the monitor are out of time with each other. Then *"Stay with your old dad, lad,"* and a shoulder turns at the foot of the bed.
3. **35, the silent aircraft.** You can hear the joins tick.
4. **12, "the hum you've heard all night was never an aircraft."** A great retroactive chill, ruined by the Kite line.
5. **197, the lift.** It goes down to 0, waits, and comes back up.
6. **25, the loop.** *"This time, you can read it,"* and Sam's name has been started.
7. **86, the child's face opening "the way a zip opens."** Every mouth says *mum*.
8. **21, the late reflection.**

## 5. Lore

**What hooks me:**
- **Dad, 1998, seat 1A, DID NOT BOARD.** Six-year-old you on the phone (57), *"Turned round at a gate once"* (137) and your March turn-around (163).
- **Walter and Ruth.** Ruth "always made a sign", and the thing holds an arrivals sign. I only clocked that this time.
- **The child in the machine.** The child's hand on the sign (16, 2) and *BEHIND THE WHISKY* in "round and careful" capitals (139) both point at six-year-old you.
- **193:** the sign calls Sam in "the way Dad called him in from the garden."

**What falls flat:**
- **1987.** Whose date is 14/03/1987? Kite is 34 and Dad was born in 1952. One planted clue would turn the sting into lore.
- **4's** *"...not hers... should have kept..."* goes nowhere.
- **Still no found documents.** There's no clipping, no CCTV log, and neither run opened the newspapers at 52.

## 6. Boredom points

- Gate 0, from the queue to the bridge.
- 61 on every return, with the same escalator line.
- The identical opening four sections in both runs.
- *"If Sam is with you"* everywhere, when he always is.
