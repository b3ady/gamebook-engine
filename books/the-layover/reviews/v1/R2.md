# R2 — THE LAYOVER: Game and Flow Review

## 1. Verdict — 6/10

The writing is far better than this structure deserves. Underneath it the book has a hub that charges the reader for every return, Sam-overlays that contradict the text above them, deaths that cost nothing, and an ending that ignores the biggest thing the reader did.

## 2. Top 7 problems (worst first)

**1. SAM_TAKEN is bolted on after prose that has Sam standing there.**
Where: 2, 115, 72, 172, 29, 156, 53, 135, 62, 183, 117, 49, 100, 79, 93, 48. The worst case: 75 ends with Sam gone, then 2 says *"Sam stands beside you and waits for you to choose,"* and only after that comes *"If you have the codeword SAM_TAKEN, no one is waiting."* In 53 the reader watches Sam take four goes to light a candle, then is told he isn't there. In 172, *"Two green dots blink side by side… Sam takes a step to the left,"* and only then the correction.
Why it matters: the reader's most important state change is undone in the very next section, every time. The Sam-lost route reads like a patch over the main route.
Fix: write two separate blocks: "If Sam is with you…" and "If you have SAM_TAKEN…". Hotel sections 175, 151, 181, 90 and 145 already do this correctly, so copy that pattern.

**2. The Atrium hub charges four sections and 1 Fear per return.**
Where: every return runs 72 → 29 (or 172) → 156 → 115. Run 5 reads 115 four times; run 3b reads 115 five times and 72, 29 and 156 three times each, about 18% of the playthrough. The board is described in full in 72, again in 172, and again in 115, back to back. The father's-voice scene in 29 (*"Leave Sam with us"*) is a one-off set piece, but it replays word for word with the same choice. 156 adds *"Gain 1 Fear"* each time, so doing the right thing slowly drives the reader towards Final Call.
Fix: 29 fires once (codeword HEARD). Merge 72 into 115 as a two-line "return" paragraph that changes with each visit. Make the Fear in 156 one-time only. Cut the board description in 115 down to the choices.

**3. The ending hangs on one Act 1 line and ignores the Act 3 rescue.**
Where: 166 → 40 (*"Calm down. You're overreacting."*) means no TRUCE. That one choice costs the reader at 124 (the shove), sets up the Fortune roll at 193 that loses Sam, and then at 76 → 43 Sam walks away anyway. Meanwhile 20 has Sam saying *"You came back for me,"* and that is recorded nowhere.
Why it matters: one early choice punishes the reader three times, and the hardest win in the book (breaking Rule 5 while obeying Rule 1) counts for nothing.
Fix: 20 should grant TRUCE, or a new codeword such as CAME_BACK, and 76 should check for it. Drop either the 124 penalty or the 193 penalty.

**4. Deaths cost nothing, and the Handbook turns choices into a compliance quiz.**
Where: 25, 162, 45 and 11 all end *"Return to X and choose again."* Each one is the option the Handbook named one page earlier: *"Never answer the Announcer,"* *"Never ride a walkway that runs backwards,"* *"Gate 00 does not exist."*
Why it matters: with a two-way choice, a free rewind just means picking the other option. There's no tension, and the choice was obvious anyway.
Fix: make deaths cost something (+1 Claim, or the clock moves forward, or send the reader back to a real checkpoint). Add choices where obeying the rule is wrong or expensive. 47 → 61 already does this, and it's the best choice in the book.

**5. The rules pages have holes.**
- Story Mode rolls no stats, but the Fear limit is "Nerve plus 3". Story Mode readers have no limit.
- The rules say *"Turn back to the last section marked with the ✈ checkpoint symbol,"* but no ✈ appears anywhere in either run.
- Combat doesn't say what happens on a tie.
- The rules never say whether codewords carry over at loop 16 ("Turn to section 1 and begin again").
- In Story Mode, the Nerve tests at 125, 157 and 61 are decoration.

Fix: set fixed Story Mode stats, print the checkpoints, add one line each on ties and on what carries over after a loop.

**6. Some texts assume a route the reader didn't take.**
- 173: *"You'd forgotten how tall he is. The Night Manager…"* Neither reader ever met him.
- 73: *"exactly where the map promised"*. Run 5 never got the MAP.
- 13: *"Walk it clean three times"*. Run 3b never saw the notice at 129.
- 39 (SAM_TAKEN): *"Sam is still asleep wherever they've put him"*. He went through a door into white light at 75.
- 99: *"you have seen this list once already"*, even when the LAST_PAGE came from the Food Court.
- 80: *"Whether you beat it back or simply stepped round it"*.
- 34: *"Sam's old voicemail, the one you never played"*. It was Dad's.

Fix: gate each of these lines behind the codeword for the route it assumes, or rewrite it so it doesn't depend on the route.

**7. The "spot the anomaly" corridor puzzle appears three times, and failing it is tedious.**
Where: 143/138, 129–113, 13–66. After a failure you go back to the intro (129 or 13) and re-read the full inventory word for word. The anomaly at 146 is the same every time, so the claim that *"The corridor doesn't reward memory"* is false: the retry is pure memory.
Fix: keep the puzzle in 129–113 and at the jet bridge, and cut the 143/138 instance down to a teaser. After a failure, send the reader straight to the first walk, not the intro. Vary the anomaly on a retry (pick it with a Fortune roll, or move it with a codeword).

## 3. Merge / cut list

- **1 + 21**: 1 is a one-way lead-in.
- **19 + 83**: the scene is split mid-moment (*"reads over your shoulder. Turn to 83"*).
- **131 into 115**: otherwise the board is described twice in a row.
- **141 + 24**, **51 + 183 + 117**: 183's only option is *"If you read it"*. **39 + 145** if 145 has no other entry point.
- **185 → 3 → 173 → 98 → 91**: five sections of conditional jumps with no choice. Make it one ledger section that checks the codewords in a list.
- **72 into 115; 156 into 29.** See problem 2.
- **172 / 122**: both run the "dot one room behind you" beat. Keep one.
- **121 / 79**: two "grey man grown into his chair" exposition scenes. Combine them, or make them different.
- **Mechanics to drop or simplify:**
  - CLEARED_C/F/O: 115 checks items, not these codewords.
  - ECHO: it exists only to route 72 to 172 once.
  - Fortune: rolled once in 188 sections read.
  - The unenforced *"If you haven't been into the bar yet"* in 175: give it a codeword or cut it.

## 4. Flow breaks

- **75→2**: Sam is taken, then "Sam stands beside you."
- **83→15**: the reader chose "get the two of you moving"; 15 opens with nobody moving.
- **131→115, 72→172→115**: the board is described two or three times in a row.
- **72→29→156 (SAM_TAKEN)**: "Sam, three steps away… doesn't turn round"; "walk over to Sam… He glances at you."
- **2→53, 115→135→62 (SAM_TAKEN)**: Sam lights the candle, names the ward, his stomach growls.
- **51→183**: "Whatever it cost you" (it cost nothing), and "Sam sits down beside you" while he's taken.
- **100→79→93→48 (SAM_TAKEN)**: Sam asks "Joining up how?", looks at you, and says "It's something."
- **125→39**: "asleep wherever they've put him."
- **133→71→99**: Sam is "against the lockers" and is never freed.
- **73→64→91**: the tall passenger waits at the jet bridge, then the bridge is "lit and empty", then the passenger is back on it.
- **3→173**: the Night Manager is "remembered" by readers who never met him.
- **184→13 (3b)**: the three-walks rule was never taught on this route.
- **66→80**: the text hedges between two routes.
- **16→1**: 16 says "This time, you can read it," then 1 says "too far away to read".
- **20→…→43**: the rescued Sam abandons you as if the rescue never happened.
- **43→34**: the voicemail is called Sam's instead of Dad's.

## 5. Mechanics fixes

1. **Fixed Story Mode stats** (for example Nerve 9, Vigour 20, Fortune 9), so Fear and combat have a limit in Story Mode.
2. **Make time real.** Every hub return moves the clock 20 minutes. At 05:59 the gate closes. Then "fewer minutes left" in 115 is true, and dawdling in the hub costs something.
3. **Make Claim matter.** The Gate 0 ledger should check Claim. Right now 140's name-giving goes unpunished at 91 ("All debts settled") while 25 kills outright. Make 25 cost +2 Claim instead of death, and print the Claim letters on the sheet.
4. **Make deaths cost something.** A rewind also costs +1 Claim or moves the clock, or returns the reader to a printed ✈.
5. **Use in-world codeword names.** SAM_TAKEN becomes LOST, LF_USED becomes TICKET, LAST_PAGE becomes the item "Last Page". Underscored code names break the spell.
6. **Fix combat.** Add a tie rule. Raise the Unclaimed above Nerve 6, which a reader with Nerve 7–12 beats most rounds. Let the reader spend Fortune on a hit.

## 6. What works

- The Handbook is a rules sheet inside the story, and it pays off at the right moments. 47→61→20, where you break Rule 5 while obeying Rule 1, is excellent design.
- The "frightened eyes" and "Kite, not your full name" tells are fair, learnable, and used consistently (90, 29, 66).
- The hotel sections (175, 151, 181, 90, 145) handle Sam being present or absent correctly. Use them as the template.
- The voicemail timestamped 05:59, Rule 8 and the two-names-one-pass reveal are a satisfying puzzle chain.
- Choices about the brothers (166, 199, 141, 79/93) have real weight and show up again later.
