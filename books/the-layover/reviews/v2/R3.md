# R3 v2: Prose line edit, THE LAYOVER

Read in full: playthroughs 1 and 4, 120 unique sections, about 27.5k words. Counts are per unique section.

## 1. Verdict: 7.5/10 (v1: 6.5)

## 3. Top 7 language problems now

**1. LOST codas.** There are 54 of them, and 23 are built on *nobody / no one / alone*. "You'd give a great deal to hear it." (8). "You'd take that. You'd take anything." (172). "It helps less than you hoped." (140). "You can't decide whether it is cruelty or hope." (3, read 4–5 times). Each one narrates a feeling. **Rule:** one object per line, as 122 does: "The space at your elbow… is cold. You fold your arms over it."

**2. Speaking to objects.** There are 19: "he says, to the board" (140, 167, 180), "to the page" (139), "to the box" (192), "to the glass" (77), "to the perfume counter" (57), "to his knees" (181), "to the bridge" (159), and "aloud to nobody" (59, 134, 139). **Rule:** keep six, all Sam's.

**3. Everything is warm.** "Warm" appears 33 times. Twelve objects are warm when touched: the pass, receipt, passport, cup ×2, map, pen, watch, push plate, voucher, and the uniform ×2. The same simile appears twice: "as though a hand has only just let go" (132) and "as if someone… has only just taken theirs away" (157). "Warm, like a hand" (20) is back. **Rule:** keep the voucher, passport and watch.

**4. Counting, losing count, starting again.** There are about 20 counting beats. "Lose count and start again" appears five times (74, 38, 22, 192, 39), which was my v1 edit 8 cloned. **Rule:** counting belongs to the Chef and the manifest only.

**5. The narrator defends the stat.** "It's the first thing tonight that has done what you expected. The place has rules, and you are learning them. Lose 1 Fear." (109). "It is the first way out anyone has offered you all night." (85). "It's the first rest either of you has had all night." (181). In hub 3: "where it stands depends on what you carry: the BOARDING PASS…".

**6. Stock stage directions.** Sam without TRUCE has "hands in his pockets" 12 times and walks "a pace ahead" 7 times. "Not looking at you" appears 14 times. Six staff are at the end of a "shift". "Level" is used for Sam's voice four times.

**7. Choppy rhythm from over-cutting.** Paragraphs of four words or fewer rose from 73 to 86: "Not a name. Just that." (73), "Your gate area." (13). The Ruth payoff is flattened into reported speech: "'Ruth,' you say, and tell her he tried" (15). In 88, a rule sits in the prose: "If a scrap of paper tonight called you a transfer passenger…". Objects breathe too (57, 113, 118, 128, 134), which dilutes the monster.
