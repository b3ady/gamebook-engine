## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The horror choices decide nothing.**
*Where:* 90, 185, 67. P6 answers the Announcer (74), checks in (182), signs in blood (44), bluffs a pass in its own name (187) and signs again (174), for a Claim of 5+. P1's Claim is 2–3. Both readers get "*You can read most of your name on its sign now*" and "*All debts settled*". At 67 the ending branches only on TOLD and TRUCE.
*Why:* if giving the monster your name costs nothing, the book has no horror stakes. Claim reaches one scene (186).
*Fix:* key 90 and 194 to Claim bands (0–2, 3–5, 6+). At 6+ the sign is complete and the bridge costs something: Sam's initial appears under yours, or a wrong walk is fatal instead of a reset.

**2. Sam's arc is settled by 120.**
*Where:* TRUCE comes at 120, the seventh section. After that, P1 has Sam touching you at 94, 179, 181, 180, 122, 140, 121, 177, 126, 159, 110, 88 and 77. At 51 the book says "*Note the codeword TRUCE*" to a reader who already has it, so the best repair scene does nothing.
*Why:* the estrangement is the spine. It is resolved 7% of the way in, and every touch after that comes cheap.
*Fix:* make TRUCE a 0–3 track, earned at 120, 51 and 183/178. Touch only at 2+.

**3. The scaffolding has become the texture.**
*Where:* P1 prints 37 "*codeword LOST*" lines. Without TRUCE, Sam has one gesture, "*hands in his pockets*", used 10 times. 89 even uses italic headers: "*If Sam is with you:*".
*Why:* every paragraph written for a route the reader isn't on breaks the spell.
*Fix:* give LOST its own short sections at 113, 122, 97, 89, 115 and 90, and strip it from every other shared section. Write three different gestures for Sam without TRUCE.

**4. The hubs still get re-read.**
*Where:* 3 (310 words) ×4 and 153 (180 words) ×5. 122 is reprinted in full. 57 → 55 → 57 runs back to back. That is 11% of P1. 3 always drops into 153, so every return goes through two hubs.
*Why:* tension resets on every return, and the fix to 3 is buried in a frame that never changes.
*Fix:* merge 3 into 153. Give return visits to 122 and 57 a 40-word version.

**5. Leftover lore contradictions.**
*Where:* P6 holds two 05:59 voicemails (111 and 193), which makes ledger line 04 ambiguous. 187 prints 0A early. At 193, Sam has walked off up the bridge (163) yet "*boards last*", "*Twenty rows*" back, skipping both 0B and the three-walk rule. The "*small one with bitten nails*" on the sign (3, 194) matches Sam's hand at 94 and is never explained.
*Fix:* make 111 a missed call. Have 187 print "*SEAT 1A*". Give Sam one line at 193 about how he got through. Pay off the nails or cut them.
