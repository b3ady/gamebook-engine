## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The Claim limit is broken in the book's own test run.**
*Where:* run 5. 65, 92, 189, 100 and 68 bring Claim to **5 at 68**, halfway through, yet the run carries on to Claim 7 and the loop ending. Rules: *"If your Claim reaches 5, turn at once to Final Call."* At the loop the reader *"keeps your Claim,"* so a loop-two reader starts already dead. 3b reaches Claim 4 for certain (171, 97, 162, 194), and 5 if the reader watches at 22.
*Why it matters:* the five-deaths route can't be played, and the rescue route hangs on one Act 1 choice (the VOUCHER).
*Fix:* add a printed check at 3 ("If your Claim is 5 or more, turn to Final Call"). Then either raise the limit to 7 or have the loop reset Claim to 2. Rebalance the forced +1 options.

**2. Checkpoints don't exist, and some deaths cost the same as surviving.**
*Where:* no section header carries a ✈. At 194, a LOST reader without INCIDENT chooses between 162 (death, +1 Claim, back to 194) and *"eyes on it… add 1 to your Claim."* The two are identical.
*Fix:* mark 153, 90 and 110 with ✈. Send deaths back to the last ✈ so the reader loses real progress. Make 162 cost 2.

**3. The hub repeats itself and its clock is fake.**
*Where:* 3→153 every return. 3 says *"The board clatters and settles"*, then 153 says *"The board turns over… with its long dry clatter."* With all three items, 3 has the passenger *"at the foot of the escalator you came down,"* then 153 says *"The escalators stand stopped… you let it."* 194 always says 05:00, however many laps you took.
*Fix:* merge 153's menu into 3. With three items, 3 routes straight to 194. Each return advances a written clock by 20 minutes, and 194 checks it.

**4. Choices are still a compliance quiz, and the bridge gives its answers away.**
*Where:* 92, 189 and 100 are each a printed rule, offered as one of two options, with a rewind. 106 says *"Nothing is out of place"*, 75 hangs Dad's coat on a hook and 49's poster is faceless. None needs a careful eye.
*Fix:* subtler anomalies, picked by a Fortune roll on a retry. Add two more choices where obeying costs you, like 102 and 168.

**5. Continuity breaks**: the voicemail is Dad's at 111 and Sam's at 200. At 166 Sam is *"ten paces behind"* even with TRUCE, then at 101 he is *"at your side."* 189 has the *"young man in the paper hat"* waving, but readers who went 111 never met him. 41 says *"This time, you can read it"*, then 1 says *"too far away to read."* 75 has *"If you went up to Floor 0"* with no codeword behind it.
*Fix:* a TRUCE branch at 166; codewords KIOSK and FLOOR0; a loop codeword for 1; one owner for the voicemail.

## 5. Mechanics notes

- **Claim:** It does real work now (the letters on the sign, the Claim-3 check in Ground Crew, the cost of a death). But the limit is never checked, and the ledger ignores the name given at 171.
- **Fear:** Healthy. Run 5 reaches about 10, which is Final Call at Nerve 7. 58 and 126 give relief at the right moments.
- **Deaths and checkpoints:** Well written, but each death rewinds one section, and ✈ is never printed.
- **Story Mode:** Fixed. Fixed stats, and Claim still bites. The Nerve tests are honestly labelled as choices.
- **Ledger:** Good structure, with debts paid through choices (39, 123, 84/50/25/9). It's repetitive and ignores Claim. The skip at 84 hands TRUCE yet another reward (it already gates 16, 67 and 194), so one line at 54 still decides too much.
- **Hub escalation:** Tying the passenger's position at 3 to the item count is the best change in v2. The clock is cosmetic.
- **Combat/Fortune:** The Unclaimed (Nerve 7) is still easy. Fortune: one test in 3b, none in run 5.
