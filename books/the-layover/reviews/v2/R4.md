## 3. Top 5 problems now

1. **"Your full name, every part" is the new logo.** It turns up in 43, 158, 92, 189, 169, 102, 83, 182, 174, 38 and 80. Chilling at 158; wallpaper by 102. Keep it for the Announcer and the Kite reveal (169).
2. **The hubs are copy-paste.** Hubs 153, 3 and 14 repeat verbatim. Exit 8's rule: repetition only scares when something changes. Make 153 change.
3. **Deaths have no ceiling.** Claim 6, nothing changed. Give Claim a threshold: at 5 the sign is finished, and that's an ending.
4. **The fights are still there.** 114 has the best creature design in the book (*"the card is blank. It wants it filled in"*), and you beat it with dice. Turn both fights into chases with a cost.
5. **The win condition is spelled out at 85.** *"IT CAN'T COME WHERE ONE WAS SAID."* I knew the answer long before 38. Blur it.

## 4. Scariest moments, ranked

1. **194 and 3: the bitten nails.** *"Among the fingers holding the sign is a child's hand, the nails bitten down."* At 94 it's Sam's hand in the dark, *"the nails bitten down to nothing."* Sam is standing next to you. Nobody says it out loud. Best thing in the revision.
2. **68: the mirror swap.** *"Sam makes a joke about the sunglasses, and it laughs half a second early... Then it puts a hand on his shoulder, and he lets it."*
3. **94: the choir.** *"many breaths at once, slightly out of time with each other, like a choir that has never rehearsed,"* then the aftershave, then Sam's hand.
4. **92: the PA.** *"Maintenance closes the ticket. NO FAULT FOUND. The name goes on."*
5. **162: the pen.** *"Behind you, close to your ear, a felt pen squeaks once on card, finishing a letter."*
6. **136: the hospital room.** *"You saw it once, in the corner of a video call, for four minutes, before you said the signal was going."* And at 102: *"Its chest rises. The monitor beeps... The two are not keeping time."*

## 5. Lore

**What hooks me:**
- **The steady handwriting.** Rule 0's line is *"smaller and rounder and steadier"* (85), and the breath-message at 24 is *"Round and careful and steady."* Same hand, never confirmed. Essays incoming.
- **LV 1987.** The receipt says 14/03/1987, and then a whole new flight is delayed.
- **The ledger (91).** *"04 · CORRESPONDENCE · 1 MSG · 05:59 · UNHEARD."* A document that *is* the plot.
- **The Night Manager's register** (174): *"One near the top in a child's careful capitals."*
- **The secret ending:** three scattered clues, and 88 never prints the number.

**What falls flat:**
- 38's narrated reveal.
- Three Announcer voices, but nothing contradicts, so nothing to argue about.
- Still no clipping, CCTV log or PA transcript.

## 8. What works

- The entity is finally a presence, not a wiki page. Staged reveals (65 → 3 → 194) actually escalate.
- The deaths are distinct and quotable: the PA, the walkway that takes you back to the viewing deck (189), the endless jet bridge (100), the mirror, the pen.
- The Kite tell (169): *"It doesn't know that."*
- Sam's jokes now die out as things get worse, exactly as they should.
- The ending no longer resolves cleanly.
