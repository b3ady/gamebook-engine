## 3. Top 5 problems now

**1. Arrival still over-ends.** This is the third review to flag it. "Or I'm a liar" is the line. After it come "I can't be the only one left", the knee bump, "Took you long enough", the lean, and a speakerless "Safe landings, Kite." That is four endings stacked up. *Fix:* cut the explaining sentence and the lean. Put "Safe landings, Kite" on the tape as Dad's, before "Has Kite landed?". End on "Took you long enough."

**2. In Separate Seats the lie never comes due.** The reader said "Nothing. No signal" (142) and "Nothing" again (36). At 14, Sam says Dad "never told us why" while the reader holds the phone that does tell, and stays silent. The FORGOT clause in 124 claims the reader knows a recording they have never played. *Fix:* in 124, have the reader say "I had signal. At the gate. I didn't want to hear it." before "It's for both of us". For FORGOT, change the clause to "You think of the jet bridge getting it exactly right, and wonder if this is the only other place it's kept."

**3. Route 6 spends Sam's best line twice.** 199 has "I meant it for about a year. Then I didn't know how to stop meaning it." 14 repeats it almost word for word, and "Don't bother coming back" is quoted three times (178, 199, 14). The second time, it reads as the author repeating himself, not Sam. *Fix:* in 14, if you've been through 199: "You heard me own it. Back there. You still didn't come."

**4. The name goes to the wrong Sam.** Cold Sam says "Kite" three times at 95. Warm Sam never says it, and the True ending's last word has no speaker. *Fix:* keep 95. In 150, after the postcards, add: "'Kite's in Lisbon,' he'd tell them."

**5. The hubs repeat themselves and miss state.** 48 prints the identical thumb-in-the-0 beat four times in route 6, with no revisit skip. It also has no MARKED clause, though MARKED Sam hides that hand everywhere else. The SIGNED loop at 74 can take Kite twice. *Fix:* give 48 a revisit skip and the clause "*If MARKED,* he uses the other thumb." Signing at 74 should keep SIGNED.

**Sam.** He's stronger than in v4. 163 ("You did the funeral flowers by email") finally gives cold Sam an accusation that is his own. 199's "stops dead" is the best beat in the book. 14 over-explains: "You got to a gate and turned round on him" spells out a rhyme the reader already sees. Cut it.

**The reader.** The "Nothing" pattern (142, 36) is now a real trait: lying by withholding. Alone pays it off ("You don't play that one either"). Separate Seats has to as well. See #2.

**Kite.** The name is now the book's spine: 115 doesn't know it, 133 takes it, the jet bridge gets it "exactly right", the tape returns it. Only #4 and the orphan last line undercut it.

**Dad.** The voicemail now explains 42's buried memory of the phone "too big for your hand". That's beautifully seeded. On cold routes he is still flawless. Put "Brave man. Scared of a phone call." into 163.

**Hostess.** 89 ("tired, about fifty") gives her a face. "He always has the window" (18) sets up both Alone and 200. Very good.

**Walter.** Pick one payoff: Ruth or the phone call.
