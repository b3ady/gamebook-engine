## 3. Top 7 problems

**1. The neutral text is lying again.**
- **54:** Path 4 phones the aunt (142) and never visits the kiosk, yet reads "Your order, line for line" and "The receipt in your pocket… says 1987 too". Sam asks about a hot chocolate he never had.
- **153:** "It has never been this close without a wall between you." False on both paths: it brushed your cheek at 141 and sat in an open lift at 19.
- **138 → 174:** Sam is already through, holding the door with his heel. Then it "slides shut in your face" and "opens for Sam", who "stops on the threshold".
- **92 → 136:** At 92 Sam takes the pass for every reader. So "If Sam took the pass from your hand… Kind ones stay" fires for everyone.

**2. Two more are "says it again" (120, 81), and Hollowmouth carries three jokes (156, 171, 58). Better variety, but still a quota.

**3. Each is good; together they're a formula. On path 1, 42 and 21 also repeat one reveal ("four months", "so you wouldn't worry").

**4. Frequency words untouched.** Hands 99, eyes 48, still 39, breath 56, "If Sam is with you" 50.

**5. The ending image is spent early.** 56 and 90 both use frightened eyes before 136.

## 5. Ten line edits

1. **54:** "Your order, line for line." → "Somebody's order, line for line." Move "The receipt in your pocket… says 1987 too" and Sam's hot-chocolate line into a block: *If you bought food at the gate kiosk.*
2. **153:** Cut "It has never been this close without a wall between you."
3. **174:** "You step towards the open door, and it slides shut in your face." → "Sam is through. As you reach the door, it slides shut between you." Cut "The door opens for Sam. He stops on the threshold, looks back at you, and waits." → "On the far side, Sam waits."
4. **136:** "If Sam took the pass from your hand" → "If you held the pass out to Sam at the desk".
5. **25:** "In the black glass, where three reflections stood, there are two and a half. Yours is the faint one." → "In the black glass, your reflection has gone faint."
6. **120:** "people who have stood at the front of a queue for twenty-eight years and never reached the desk" → "people who have queued for twenty-eight years and never reached the desk."
7. **56:** "His eyes. Up close you can see them properly. They are frightened." → "His eyes don't blink."
8. **90:** "His kind brown eyes are as frightened as all of theirs." → "His kind brown eyes."
9. **42:** Cut "Four months he knew before he told you. So you wouldn't worry." 21 does it better.
10. **18:** Merge "Good evening, madam." into the next paragraph (third request).
