## 3. Top 5 problems now

1. **Claim still gets wiped.** It's smaller now, but a reckless run that died twice still gets the same stamp, the same seats and the same bridge. Give high Claim a cost you can see: Sam's name only half printed on the pass, or the coat at 90 using your face as well as Dad's.
2. **Exposition.** The grey man and the Hostess at 89 read you the rules before you can work them out. The scariest sections (99, 60, 34) explain nothing.
3. **The Gate 0 desk is still slow, and it has a bug.** 160 prints in full twice in both runs. At 74, *"cross off SIGNED"* should say "note SIGNED". As printed, signing there leaves you open to 8 again.
4. **The secret is over-clued.** That's a tag, a cigarette packet, page small print and pass small print, all for "turn to the flight number". Cut two of them, and cut the guard line at 137 that admits the trick.
5. **Repetition.** 171 prints six times per run, and in P5 it printed twice back to back after 132. With the motifs and the full-name tic, you can predict every beat.

## 4. Scariest moments, ranked

1. **60, the hospital bed.** *"Its chest rises. The monitor beeps. Its chest falls. The two are not keeping time."* At 181 a shoulder turns under the sheet at the foot of the bed. And 76: *"You saw it once, in the corner of a video call, for four minutes, before you said the signal was going."*
2. **185, the loop.** *"This time, you can read it."* Sam's name has been started underneath yours.
3. **99, the break room.** The staff sit facing the wall, and in the dark you hear *"the sound chairs make when the people in them start, slowly, to turn."*
4. **34, the faceless poster.** Smooth skin from hairline to chin, and *"the smaller child's arm has come down. It is pointing at you."* It's perfect Exit 8.
5. **94.** The pen *"squeaks again, and starts the next."*
6. **40.** *"NO FAULT FOUND. The name goes on."*
7. **90.** An old man with peppermint on his breath is pressed into the coat. If you promised Walter, that's him.
8. **104.** *"Every mouth is saying mum."*

## 5. Lore

**What hooks me:**
- **The theory finally fits together.** You were six in 1998 (the radio at 145). At 42 there's a memory of a kitchen at night and a phone too big for your hand, with you crying. The manifest says Dad *DID NOT BOARD*. At 137 he says he *"turned round at a gate once."* So you rang him crying and he didn't get on LV 137. The child's hand on the sign (153), the child's capitals in the register (74) and the *"round and careful"* breath writing (127) could all be you. None of it is spelled out. Good.
- **Walter:** 113 → 165 → 20 → 90 → 137 is still the best throughline.

**What falls flat:**
- **The voicemail.** Ledger line 04 says *"Not at this desk."* Neither run could play it. Hidden branch or loose thread?
- There's still no CCTV log or news clipping as found text. Neither run opened the newspapers at 130.

## 8. What works

- **The 171 progress meter.** The tall passenger edging closer as you collect items is the best new mechanic.
- **The 17 confession and the payoff at 137.** *"I got as far as the gate"* answered by *"Turned round at a gate once"* is the emotional peak of the book.
- **Sam.** *"We're fine x"* (178), *"Breathe when I do"* (60), *"I'm not done being angry"* (17).
- **The Kite tell** at 115 is still the best rule in the book, because it isn't printed in the Handbook.
- **The jet bridge's anomalies.** The coat on the hook, then the faceless poster, is Exit 8 done right.
