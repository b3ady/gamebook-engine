## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The hub problem moved; it wasn't solved.**
*Where:* 171 ×6 on both paths. P6's hotel is still 16 sections with three lift rides, and 102 dumps you back at the lobby (146). Even with the stub, every 171 visit makes the reader parse three passenger-position variants.
*Why:* each Atrium lap resets dread. "*A little higher than before*" stops landing by the fourth time.
*Fix:* split 171 into a first visit and a 40-word "Atrium, again" section. Send 102 to 194 or 132. Route 161 to the lifts without going back through the lobby.

**2. 05:59 is a clock that never ticks.**
*Where:* the deadline is on every board, and 111 says "*You have lost time you never spent*". But there's no clock on the Adventure Sheet, and nothing on either path spends time. Fear changes a dozen times and never gates a choice. Vigour is lost (176, 70, 74) and never checked.
*Why:* laps are free, so wandering is free. The prose promises a deadline the mechanics don't keep.
*Fix:* add a TIME track. Each 171 lap ticks it, and thresholds move the passenger and add a ledger line. Let high Fear lock out the eyes-shut options at 153 and 43.

**3. The ledger is still a queue, and now it has a rules bug.**
*Where:* signing at 74 does "*cross off SIGNED, and turn to 160*". With Claim 3+, that re-fires 8 → 133, so "*the short one*" gets taken twice. 74 also checks "*If Sam crossed your name out of the ledger*", which has no codeword.
*Why:* on P6 the climax is accounting, with one real choice (74) and one dice roll (86).
*Fix:* signing should add a cost and exit, not loop. Give the CARE strike its own codeword (STRUCK). Give 182 a non-TRUCE option that costs Vigour.

**4. Sam is bookkept four ways, and P6 hears one beat twice.**
*Where:* THAW adds lines at 141, 166, 42 and 156, and stands in for TRUCE at 56. On P6, 199's "*I meant it for about a year*" comes back almost word for word in 14.
*Why:* the repeat flattens P6's best confrontation.
*Fix:* fold THAW into CARE. Keep TRUCE and TOLD. Cut the repeated line from 14.

**5. Unpaid lore.**
*Where:* "*Capt. R… Your father was Ray*" (140) and "*Captain says the seat's been empty*" (102) clash with 1A "*DID NOT BOARD*", which makes him a passenger. 1987 is still unattached, 137 is still counted three times, and the child's hand is still loose.
*Fix:* decide whether Dad was the pilot or a passenger. Attach 1987 (14/03 rhymes with your March gate) or redate it. Keep 108's count only. Tie the child's hand at 153 to 42's phone.
