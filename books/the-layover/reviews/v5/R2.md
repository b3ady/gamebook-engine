## 3. Top 5 problems

**1. The reckoning still comes after the cliff, and 3b is illegal again.**
Hand count for 3b: 159 (1), 56 (2), 94 (3), eyes-on at 153 (4), 170 (−1) = **3** at 160. That must route to 8. The generator went straight to 92. If 3b had failed the chase at 9 or watched at 43, it would hit 5 inside 153. A LOST reader can't take the TRUCE or CARE options at 153, because Sam isn't with them. At Claim 4, a failure at 118 sends them to 49, which adds 1: Final Call. The choice that loses Sam (56) also costs a Claim, so losing him is charged twice.
*Fix:* move 8's check to the top of 153, where *"If your Claim is 3 or more"* already sits. Make 56 cost nothing for a reader who goes on to LOST. Print *"If your Claim is now 5, turn to Final Call"* in 94 and 49.

**2. The hub is broken and bloated.**
171's three item clauses (none or one / two / three) cover every reader. So *"Otherwise, on your first visit"* never applies, and that is the only text that describes the three exits. The section is printed six or seven times per run.
*Fix:* put the order as first visit, then later visits, then where the passenger stands. Trigger 111 and 115 from the top of 171 itself, so every return line just says "Turn to 171".

**3. Fortune is vestigial.**
Run 5 has 0 tests. 3b has one (10) and run 4 has one. Story Mode removes even those.
*Fix:* turn the tests at 9, 29 and 112 into Fortune tests, or cut the stat.

**4. On a retry, the bridge is still a memory test.**
On a retry the anomalies come back unchanged, so anyone who reaches 112 has already solved it.
*Fix:* 112 tests Fortune. If Lucky, the same order. If Unlucky, an alternate set where the clean walk comes second.

**5. The rules still hide the face tax.**
FRONT_RULES: *"every time you give your name away, and another every time you die."* Looking costs a Claim at 187, 56, 43, 153 and 19, and so does failing the chase at 9. 85's *"go to the window to see him properly"* gives no warning.
*Fix:* add *"…and every time it learns your face."* Put 56's cost on 85's choice line.
